本届研究与论文

Learning to be Different: Component Specialization for Compositional Diffusion Policies

Poster · 首位署名

该篇个人署名单位：Harvard University

摘要原文

Diffusion policies with linearly composed scores have emerged as a promising approach to multi-task robotic learning by combining separate factors to enable modular adaptation, compositional generalization, and interpretable functional decomposition. However, current training objectives do not encourage factor specialization, often resulting in uninterpretable denoisers whose composed prediction averages over redundant copies, undermining their architectural premise. To address this, we propose a drop-in score-decorrelation auxiliary loss, grounded in projective composition theory. Our approach encourages orthogonality between component noise predictions with little architectural change or computational overhead. We demonstrate across multiple benchmarks that our decorrelation loss consistently reduces measurable double counting between components, improves held out compositional generalization, and produces behaviorally meaningful specialization without compromising multitask performance.
