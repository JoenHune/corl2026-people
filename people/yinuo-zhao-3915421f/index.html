Not All Actions are Equal: Reliable Value Estimation for Offline-to-Online Reinforcement Learning

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics

摘要原文

Offline-to-online reinforcement learning is a promising paradigm for generalizable robotic manipulation, but its full-stack complexity makes system behavior difficult to reproduce and diagnose. Within such systems, value estimation plays a central role by determining how heterogeneous data are prioritized for policy improvement. Despite its importance, a central question remains underexplored: how value-function reliability affects value-guided learning across offline pretraining and online adaptation. To answer this question, we ground our analysis in a complete offline-to-online reinforcement learning pipeline and introduce value-reliability metrics that quantify value-function quality along global task-level progress and local action-level preference, enabling us to analyze how value estimation affects downstream policy behavior. Across the extensive empirical study encompassing 240 hours of pretraining data and over 3,000 rollout trajectories, our experiments show that these metrics are closely aligned with downstream performance: More reliable value functions yield better action-quality estimates, enabling value-guided offline RL to scale more effectively than quality-agnostic behavior cloning. As value estimates become more reliable, online adaptation becomes more stable through better prioritization of high-quality rollout data. Integrating reliable value guidance through offline pretraining with online adaptation, our system achieves 86\% success on millimeter-level precise chip insertion and 84\% on generalizable block disassembly. Ultimately, we hope our findings could underscore the importance of value-guided data utilization for turning heterogeneous robotic experience into effective policy improvement. We will release the full offline-to-online RL framework and the corresponding pretraining data to promote reproducibility and further research.

RoboAug: One Annotation to Hundreds of Scenes via Region-Contrastive Data Augmentation for Robotic Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics / City University of Hong Kong

摘要原文

Enhancing the generalization of robotic learning in diverse unseen environments remains a fundamental challenge. Existing approaches often rely on large-scale pretraining, which is labor-intensive and time-consuming, or semantic data augmentation methods that assume flawless upstream object detection in real-world scenarios. In this work, we propose RoboAug, a novel generative data augmentation framework that reduces reliance on large-scale pretraining and perfect visual recognition by requiring only a single image with bounding box annotations for dataset construction. Leveraging this minimal supervision, RoboAug employs pretrained generative models for precise semantic augmentation and introduces a plug-and-play region-contrastive loss to guide attention toward task-relevant regions, thereby enhancing generalization and task success rates. We conduct extensive real-world experiments on three robotic platforms—UR-5e, AgileX, and Tien Kung 2.0—covering over 35k rollouts. Experimental results show that RoboAug consistently outperforms state-of-the-art augmentation baselines. In unseen environments with varying backgrounds, distractors, and lighting conditions, RoboAug improves success rates over the no-augmentation baseline from 0.09 to 0.47 on UR-5e, 0.16 to 0.60 on AgileX, and 0.19 to 0.67 on Tien Kung 2.0. These results demonstrate the superior generalization and effectiveness of RoboAug in real-world manipulation tasks.
