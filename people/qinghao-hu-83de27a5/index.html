本届研究与论文

ACE-VLA: Action Centric Evaluation for Token Pruning in Vision-Language-Action Models

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Pruning redundant visual tokens is a promising direction for reducing the high inference cost of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. However, existing pruning criteria are largely inherited from generic Vision-Language Models (VLMs), relying on vision-text attention or static semantics that struggle to adapt to the stage-wise nature of embodied manipulation. As manipulation progresses, relevant objects and interaction regions evolve, requiring token importance to shift accordingly. To address this, we observe that a policy’s predicted actions naturally reflect short-horizon task progression and interaction dynamics. Based on this insight, we propose the Action-Centric Evaluation (ACE) framework, a training-free pruning approach that scores visual tokens by their influence on action generation. ACE estimates token importance by back-attributing predicted actions to visual tokens via gradient-weighted action relevance, enabling stage-aware visual token pruning for embodied control. Experiments show that ACE reduces FLOPs by up to 39.0%. On π-series backbones, ACE improves average success rates by 1.7%–2.0% while achieving 1.33× speedup on LIBERO and 1.36× in real-world tasks. On OpenVLA-OFT, ACE preserves competitive performance with 1.57× and 1.55× speedups in simulation and real-world settings, respectively. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of action-centric pruning for efficient embodied control. Codes are available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/ACE-VLA-5A29/.
