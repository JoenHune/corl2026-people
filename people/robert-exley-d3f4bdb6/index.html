AmodalBox: Automated 3D Bounding Box Annotation under Partial Visibility

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Recent progress in vision foundation models (VFMs) has made automated open-vocabulary 3D bounding box annotation increasingly feasible by jointly leveraging LiDAR point clouds and images. However, partial observation remains a major challenge in sparse driving scenes, where visible LiDAR points often cover only a small fraction of the object's surface and provide insufficient cues for estimating its full spatial extent. As a result, existing methods often produce mislocalized boxes or leave objects unlabeled. To address this limitation, we propose AmodalBox, an automated annotation framework that leverages image-generated 3D assets as amodal geometric priors for partially observed objects. By establishing correspondences between input images and rendered views of 3D assets, AmodalBox robustly aligns canonical amodal geometry with partially observed world-space LiDAR point clouds. To improve temporal consistency and reduce computational cost, AmodalBox propagates anchor-based alignments for dynamic instances and aggregates multi-frame evidence for static objects. 3D object detectors trained on annotations from AmodalBox improves performance over strong baselines by 14.1 mAP on the nuScenes dataset and by 12.45 mAP at IoU 0.5 for the vehicle class on the Waymo Open Dataset (WOD).
