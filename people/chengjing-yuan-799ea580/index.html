本届研究与论文

XDex: Learning Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Grasping with 1000 Hands

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Generalizing dexterous grasping across diverse robot hands remains a fundamental challenge due to differences in geometry, topology, and kinematics. Inspired by recent advances in scaling up robot data, we show that scaling both the diversity and quantity of hand embodiments significantly improves generalization to unseen morphologies. We present XDex, a unified cross-embodiment grasp synthesis framework trained on the largest and most diverse multi-hand grasp dataset to date, spanning 1,000 heterogeneous robotic hands and 7k object scenes. XDex couples an embodiment encoder that jointly captures geometric and topological features with a three-way disentangled representation of object geometry, hand embodiment, and grasping pose. It outperforms prior methods in grasp quality, consistency, and diversity, and generalizes to unseen hands in real-world settings.
