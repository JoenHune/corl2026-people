本届研究与论文

CoSMO: Conformal Semantic Mapping for Open-World Safe Robot Navigation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Fudan University

摘要原文

Safe autonomous navigation requires not only geometric obstacle avoidance but also semantic awareness. Different object categories demand different safety clearances, and the set of relevant categories may expand as the robot explores novel environments. Vision-language models (VLMs) offer rich semantic representations for building maps of unknown scenes, yet their confidence scores are poorly calibrated, making raw predictions unreliable for safety-critical planning. Conformal prediction (CP) can provide coverage guarantees, but existing methods assume a fixed label set and stationary data, limiting their use in open-world settings. We propose Conformal Semantic Mapping for Open-World Safe Robot Navigation (CoSMO), a semantic safety mapping framework that transforms VLM embeddings into calibrated coverage sets and safety-aware obstacle dilations over a dynamically expanding hazard vocabulary, even under distribution shift. Experiments on the Matterport3D (MP3D) dataset show that CoSMO reduces semantic collisions by 61.6% relative to a geometry-only baseline, while maintaining formal coverage guarantees even under delayed expert feedback. Code will be released upon publication.
