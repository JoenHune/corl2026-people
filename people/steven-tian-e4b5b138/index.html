本届研究与论文

Eye, Robot: Learning Expressive Gaze Dynamics via a VLM-Guided Transformer-VAE

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Columbia University

摘要原文

Gaze plays a crucial role in human communication, conveying intent, emotion, and vitality through subtle eye and head movements. However, realistic gaze behaviors in humanoid robots are often constrained by task-specific controllers, such as object tracking systems, resulting in rigid, unnatural motion that lacks human-like expressiveness. In this work, we propose a VLM-guided framework for a facial robot that decouples high-level cognitive reasoning from low-level motor control, with a action head learned directly from human motion data. A Vision-Language Model interprets the scene and designates gaze targets that condition our action head, the Realistic Gaze Transformer (RGT). Built on a Transformer-VAE, RGT is first pre-trained on human motion sequences in a self-supervised manner to learn a compact latent representation of full-head dynamics, then fine-tuned autoregressively for stable long-horizon rollouts under closed-loop deployment. At inference, conditioned on a brief motion history and the VLM-directed eyeball trajectory, RGT generates real-time 11-DoF commands synthesizing fluid eye–head coordination, eyelid motion, and natural blinking. By separating motion realism from gaze targeting, our framework preserves task-directed gaze while enhancing naturalness, yielding robot gaze that is both purposeful and imbued with the subtle vitality of human eye contact.
