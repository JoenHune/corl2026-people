本届研究与论文

CMRDF: Morphology-Conditioned Reachability Fields for Task-Space Robot Policy Transfer

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Generalist robot policies often represent actions as end-effector target poses in task space, which facilitates policy transfer across robots performing the same task. However, a task-space pose that is feasible for one morphology may be infeasible for another due to joint limits, workspace differences, or self-collision constraints. Existing pose reachability methods often learn monolithic fields over the full SE(3) pose space, which spreads expensive inverse-kinematics supervision over a high-dimensional domain and leaves near-boundary regions poorly sampled in cross-morphology settings. We propose Cross-Morphology Reachability Distance Fields (CMRDF), a morphology-conditioned task-space reachability representation that lowers the complexity of SE(3) reachability learning by decomposing full-pose feasibility into a global position-reachability field and a local, position-conditioned orientation-reachability field. This hierarchy replaces a monolithic field over all position-orientation combinations with a dense Position-SDF over the 3D workspace and a conditional Orientation-SDF over local orientation feasibility. To align supervision with this hierarchical representation, CMRDF uses dense forward-kinematics coverage and distance-to-boundary targets for the Position-SDF, and applies inverse-kinematics boundary mining only at representative position anchors for the Orientation-SDF. The resulting differentiable distance fields provide physically meaningful margins for reachability queries, near-boundary modeling, and gradient-based repair of infeasible poses. With only 0.51x the full-pose inverse-kinematics supervision budget, CMRDF achieves 85.53% SE(3) accuracy, improving by 6.45 percentage points over the strongest evaluated 1.00x non-CMRDF baseline, while also supporting local pose repair and zero-shot transfer under structured morphology shifts.
