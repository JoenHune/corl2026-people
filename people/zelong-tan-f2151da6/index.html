本届研究与论文

Equivariant Visual-Tactile Diffusion Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Chinese University of Hong Kong

摘要原文

Imitation learning for contact-rich manipulation requires high-quality expert data that is expensive to obtain. This makes learning a spatial robust policy under limited demonstrations a key issue. To address this, we propose VISTA, an equivariant visuotactile diffusion policy for data-efficient contact-rich imitation learning. VISTA projects vision and tactile into spherical tokens, injects tactile contact cues into visual spherical directions through permutation-equivariant spherical fusion, and rotates the fused harmonic representation using the end-effector orientation. The resulting representation conditions an equivariant diffusion policy to predict spatially consistent actions. Extensive experiments in both simulation and real-world robotic settings show that VISTA substantially improves data efficiency and spatial robustness over strong visuotactile imitation learning baselines.
