本届研究与论文

OmniGuide: Universal Spatial Guidance Fields for Enhancing Generalist Robot Policies

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of Pennsylvania

摘要原文

Vision-language-action (VLA) models have shown great promise as generalist policies for a large range of relatively simple tasks. However, they demonstrate limited performance on more complex tasks, such as those requiring complex spatial or semantic understanding, manipulation in clutter, or precise manipulation. We propose OmniGuide, a flexible framework that improves VLA performance on such tasks by leveraging versatile sources of guidance, such as 3D foundation models, semantic-reasoning VLMs, and human pose models. We show how to naturally express many kinds of guidance as differentiable energy functions with task-specific attractors and repellers located in 3D space, that influence the sampling of VLA actions. In this way, OmniGuide enables complementary task-relevant guidance to improve a VLA model's performance on challenging tasks. Extensive experiments in both simulation and real-world environments, across diverse sources of guidance like collision safety and human demonstration, prove that OmniGuide enhances the performance of state-of-the-art generalist policies (e.g., $\pi_{0.5}$, GR00T N1.6) significantly in terms of both success and safety rates. Critically, our unified framework matches or surpasses the performance of prior methods designed to incorporate specific sources of guidance into VLA policies.
