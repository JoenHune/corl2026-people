本届研究与论文

WM-Craftnet: World Synesthesia Model for Robust and Generalizable Dexterous In-Hand Manipulation

Spotlight · 通讯作者 · 已核原文

该篇个人署名单位：Sharpa Robotics

把动作条件世界模型作为灵巧手策略的循环状态上下文，融合深度、触觉和本体感知。通过多物体手内旋转、扰动恢复及仿真到真机迁移研究状态表征的鲁棒性。

摘要原文

Robust and generalizable dexterous in-hand manipulation requires a policy to infer object pose, geometry, contact, and potential slip from partial and noisy observations. Although recent tactile and visuotactile RL methods achieve strong in-hand rotation in controlled settings, their robustness often degrades under pose shifts, force disturbances, and object variation. We propose WM-Craftnet, a world-model-conditioned framework that learns compact latent dynamics from proprioception, depth, tactile sensing, actions, and rewards. Rather than using the world model for latent imagination or policy optimization, WM-Craftnet uses the learned World Synesthesia Model (WSM) as recurrent task context for an asymmetric actor--critic policy. Importantly, WSM is trained to reconstruct clean depth targets from noisy depth inputs, providing a denoised geometric state for real-robot deployment. Ablations over WSM modality heads show how tactile and depth prediction shape the synesthetic state, with clean-depth supervision providing large gain, and a WSM pretrained on nine z-axis objects serves as a reusable prior for 49-object downstream policy learning. This context improves multi-object rotation, with quantitative and qualitative evidence for unseen-object and sim-to-real transfer, and qualitative recovery from challenging initial poses and external force disturbances. To benefit the research community, we will release the code upon paper acceptance.
