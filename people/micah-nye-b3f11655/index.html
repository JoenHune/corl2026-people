本届研究与论文

Structured Perception Representations for Off-road Dynamics Learning

Poster · 首位署名

该篇个人署名单位：Carnegie Mellon University

摘要原文

Autonomously navigating off-road terrain at high speed demands accurate control, which often relies on dynamics models that capture complex interactions between the robot and terrain. In off-road regimes, terrain interaction is a major component of the vehicle's dynamics. Prior work attempts to capture this effect by training learned models of the vehicle's dynamics, conditioned on its perception of the local environment. However, said conditioning is often overly-simplistic, ignoring the spatial structure of the dynamics problem. Our approach exploits this spatial structure; at each step, we query the BEV features around the predicted state, conditioning the next prediction on current geometric and semantic cues. We additionally provide a benchmarking approach to extensively evaluate dynamics models on challenging datasets collected across all seasons, decreasing the worst-case prediction accuracy by 23.8\%, compared to physics-based and learned baselines. We further evaluate on hardware on high-speed ($\geq$10m/s) and geometrically challenging courses with a 34.9\% reduction in maximum cross-track error.
