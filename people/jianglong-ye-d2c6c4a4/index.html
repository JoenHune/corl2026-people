RoboKit: Accelerating Parallel Differentiable Optimization for Robot Learning

Spotlight · 首位署名

摘要原文

We present RoboKit, a performant, general-purpose toolkit that extends the value of optimization to robot learning. It is built around three principles: (i) \emph{Performance and flexibility}: implemented in a kernel-level language, it pairs multi-seed sampling with versatile optimizers. With well-tuned engineering, it runs over $10\times$ faster than prior frameworks; (ii) \emph{Geometry and rendering}: it makes geometry and differentiable rendering first-class and accelerates both with hybrid and coarse-to-fine representations. It can optimize 1024 grasps in 2\,s; (iii) \emph{Integration with learning}: every cost is differentiable and plugs directly into learning frameworks, letting us distill optimization into policies. The learned policies run significantly faster while matching or surpassing optimization performance.

XDex: Learning Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Grasping with 1000 Hands

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Generalizing dexterous grasping across diverse robot hands remains a fundamental challenge due to differences in geometry, topology, and kinematics. Inspired by recent advances in scaling up robot data, we show that scaling both the diversity and quantity of hand embodiments significantly improves generalization to unseen morphologies. We present XDex, a unified cross-embodiment grasp synthesis framework trained on the largest and most diverse multi-hand grasp dataset to date, spanning 1,000 heterogeneous robotic hands and 7k object scenes. XDex couples an embodiment encoder that jointly captures geometric and topological features with a three-way disentangled representation of object geometry, hand embodiment, and grasping pose. It outperforms prior methods in grasp quality, consistency, and diversity, and generalizes to unseen hands in real-world settings.

HuG: Learning Humanoid Whole-Body Affordance-Aware Grasping with Active Perception

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Humanoid loco-manipulation in unstructured environments is often hindered by egocentric occlusions and the complexity of high-DOF whole-body coordination. To bridge this gap, we present a framework HuG, for Learning Humanoid Whole-Body Affordance-Aware Grasping with Active Perception. Our system enables a robot to autonomously discover and acquire objects through a hierarchical perception-action coordination approach. First, an active perception module manages the robot's viewpoint to resolve visual ambiguity by maintaining a recursive spatial belief of the target. Second, an affordance-aware alignment strategy leverages learned reachability priors to coordinate whole-body posture, ensuring both kinematic feasibility and dynamic stability for optimal manipulation. Finally, the acquisition is performed by a robust whole-body grasping policy distilled entirely from large-scale synthetic data. We demonstrate zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on a humanoid, showing that the coordination of perception and action significantly enhances the reliability and generalization of object acquisition across diverse spatial configurations.
