本届研究与论文

GROW2: Grounding Which and Where for Robot Tool Use

Poster · 首位署名

该篇个人署名单位：National University of Singapore

摘要原文

Tool use greatly expands robot capabilities in unstructured environments. Despite significant progress, most prior work assumes a pre-specified tool and struggles to ground affordances in a scene with multiple open-category objects. We present GROW$^{2}$ (GROunding Which and Where), a method for open-world affordance grounding. Our key insight is that object parts naturally represent both affordance semantics and geometric structure, allowing us to decompose affordance grounding into two levels. At the semantic level, GROW$^{2}$ leverages the reasoning capabilities of vision-language models (VLMs) to select an appropriate tool and identify task-relevant parts. At the geometric level, GROW$^{2}$ exploits the perception capabilities of vision foundation models to ground the selected parts into 3D affordance regions. GROW$^{2}$ outperforms state-of-the-art baselines on both existing affordance prediction benchmarks and our newly constructed benchmark, which introduces the additional challenge of tool selection. Moreover, in both simulation and real-world robot tool-use experiments, GROW$^{2}$ enables robots to achieve higher task success rates than the baselines.
