本届研究与论文

LiquidLab: Benchmarking Robotic Automation for Liquid Operation in Laboratory

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Realizing true end-to-end biological laboratory automation requires robotic systems that can support two essential capabilities: directly manipulating liquids, and preparing and clearing the programmable automated liquid-handler platforms through which liquids are ultimately processed. However, existing laboratory benchmarks such as AutoBio and LabUtopia focus on rigid-body instrument operations or chemistry procedures with limited fluid modeling fidelity. We introduce LiquidLab, a robotic benchmark to jointly evaluate physically grounded liquid manipulation and automated liquid-handler operation for biological laboratory automation, comprising sixteen tasks across direct liquid manipulation, platform operations, and long-horizon wet-lab protocols. We further propose a parallel SPH fluid simulation pipeline built on Genesis that enables large-scale parallel liquid simulation on a single GPU, and ground task success in particle-level volumetric criteria rather than pose proxies. Benchmarking four representative imitation-learning and vision-language-action policies reveals that current models remain far from reliable, with failures concentrated in millimeter-scale labware staging and free-surface fluid control, exposing a clear gap between current robotic capabilities and the demands of fully autonomous biology.
