本届研究与论文

Hardware-as-Regularization: Enhancing Data Consistency in Imitation Learning via an Adjustable Passive Compliant Leader Arm

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：SB Intuitions Corporation

摘要原文

While low-cost passive leader arms enable scalable data collection for imitation learning, their lack of restoring forces causes unconstrained redundant degrees of freedom, resulting in highly inconsistent joint-space trajectories. To address this without relying on expensive active actuators, we propose a novel, low-cost passive joint mechanism featuring an adjustable mechanical clutch and a 3D-printed spiral spring. This mechanism introduces ``Hardware-as-Regularization'' by providing adjustable mechanical compliance that acts as a physical prior. By naturally guiding redundant joints back to a user-defined zero-point, our system significantly improves kinematic consistency without increasing the operator's cognitive load. Experimental results demonstrate that our purely mechanical approach drastically reduces joint-angle variance compared to a naive passive arm, achieving trajectory consistency on par with a more complex, active virtual spring system. Furthermore, in downstream policy learning, our mechanism stabilizes demonstrations by eliminating manual drift compensation, thereby matching the task success rates of the active setup while preserving extreme cost-effectiveness.
