本届研究与论文

SuperSuit: An Isomorphic Bimodal Interface for Scalable Mobile Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：TARS Robotics

摘要原文

High-quality, long-horizon demonstrations remain a major bottleneck for embodied AI, especially for wheeled mobile manipulators that require tight coordination between planar locomotion and dexterous arm control. Existing teleoperation systems and wearable interfaces often suffer from cognitive decoupling, limited scalability, and inconsistent action representations across data collection modes. We present SuperSuit, a bimodal data acquisition framework that unifies robot-in-the-loop teleoperation and active human demonstration through a shared kinematic interface. Both modalities produce structurally identical joint-space trajectories, enabling direct data mixing without modifying downstream policies. For locomotion, SuperSuit maps natural human stepping to continuous planar base velocities, eliminating discrete command switches. For manipulation, it employs a strictly isomorphic wearable arm in both modes, while policy actions are formulated in a shift-invariant delta-joint representation to mitigate calibration offsets and structural compliance without inverse kinematics. Real-world experiments on long-horizon mobile manipulation tasks show 2.6$\times$ higher demonstration throughput in active mode than a teleoperation baseline, comparable policy performance when substituting teleoperation data with active demonstrations at a fixed dataset size, and monotonic performance improvement as active data volume increases. These results indicate that consistent kinematic representations across collection modalities enable scalable data acquisition for long-horizon mobile manipulation.
