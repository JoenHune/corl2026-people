本届研究与论文

PLaW-VLA: Predictive Latent World Modeling for Vision-Language-Action Policies

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Jilin University

摘要原文

World models provide predictive priors that are essential for vision-language-action (VLA) policies in long-horizon and dynamic manipulation. However, existing world-model-based VLA methods are often optimized with reconstruction-oriented objectives, which may prioritize low-level visual fidelity over task-relevant state evolution for control. We propose PLaW-VLA, a predictive latent world model for VLA policy learning. Rather than reconstructing future observations in pixel space, PLaW-VLA predicts future world states in a pretrained latent space, providing compact, semantically grounded, and dynamics-aware context for action generation. The model adopts a Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) architecture with shared causal self-attention, unifying environment understanding, latent world-state prediction, and action generation within a single sequence-modeling framework. Experiments in simulation and on real robots show that PLaW-VLA achieves state-of-the-art performance, especially on long-horizon, interaction-rich, and dynamically perturbed tasks, demonstrating the effectiveness of latent world-state prediction as a control-aligned objective for generalist robot policy learning.
