本届研究与论文

ACE-Ego: Unifying Egocentric Human and Robotic Data for VLA Pretraining

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：ACE Robotics

摘要原文

Vision-language-action (VLA) models benefit from large-scale and diverse embodied data, yet collecting scalable robot trajectories remains costly and labor-intensive. Egocentric human videos offer a complementary source of rich real-world interactions. However, jointly training on human and robot data remains challenging due to differences in embodiments, action spaces, temporal dynamics, and supervision fidelity. We introduce ACE-Ego, a VLA pretraining framework for heterogeneous embodied data. ACE-Ego bridges human and robot trajectories through unified camera-space actions, embodiment-aware morphology tokens, temporally aligned action chunking, and a reliability-aware objective for mixed-quality supervision. We instantiate on 4.53K hours of robot and simulation data together with 1.48K hours of pseudo-labeled egocentric human videos. Experiments show that incorporating high-quality human supervision consistently improves both pretraining and supervised fine-tuning. ACE-Ego achieves state-of-the-art performance on RoboCasa GR1 TableTop and RoboTwin~2.0, while also demonstrating strong transfer to real-world bimanual manipulation.
