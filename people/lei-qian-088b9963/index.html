本届研究与论文

Learning Human Intention for Robotic Manipulation

Spotlight · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：ShanghaiTech University

摘要原文

Embodied foundation models have significantly propelled the advancement of artificial general intelligence, yet they rely heavily on large-scale data. Recent studies identify human behavior as one of the most scalable sources for fostering robotic physical intelligence. However, the inherent embodiment gap remains a fundamental challenge for effective human-to-robot learning. In this work, we model human intention to enable efficient learning from human demonstrations. Among human behavioral cues, gaze simultaneously encodes high-level task semantics and exhibits a synergistic coupling with low-level action. By modeling intention through gaze, we derive a mid-level representation that facilitates cross-embodiment knowledge transfer. We demonstrate that robots can internalize human intentions to enhance manipulation, even without robotic intention annotations. To capture the synergy between intention and action, we adopt a Chain-of-Thought reasoning paradigm during inference. Our approach enables the execution of long-horizon and fine-grained tasks across diverse embodiments. Extensive experiments in both simulation and real-world demonstrate that our method consistently outperforms baseline approaches and generalizes to unseen settings.
