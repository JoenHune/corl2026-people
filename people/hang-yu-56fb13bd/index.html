本届研究与论文

DriveMLP: When Training Strategy Outperforms Model Complexity in Autonomous Driving

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Mercedes-Benz AG / Karlsruhe Institute of Technology

摘要原文

Learning motion planning policies that remain reliable under closed-loop execution is a central challenge in Autonomous Driving (AD). Imitation Learning (IL) scales well to large AD datasets but suffers from distribution shift, causal confusion, and compounding errors under closed-loop execution. In contrast, Reinforcement Learning (RL) typically requires handcrafted rule-based rewards and massive simulation. We introduce a simple closed-loop IL framework that uses RL purely as an optimization tool, guided by a trajectory-distance reward derived from logged expert data. A lightweight MLP policy trained with our method achieves a score of 86.51 on the nuPlan Val14 Reactive benchmark, a +3.2 point improvement over the previous best IL planner (Flow Planner), with >15x faster inference. Our central finding is that training strategy plays a role at least as important as model complexity: closed-loop optimization with trajectory-based supervision is sufficient to outperform recent large-scale generative IL planners while dramatically reducing inference cost. Code and trained models will be made publicly available upon acceptance.
