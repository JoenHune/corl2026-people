本届研究与论文

World In Your Hands: A Large-scale Dataset for Learning Ego-centric Robotic Manipulation In-the-Wild

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：TARS Robotics

摘要原文

In this paper, we introduce World In Your Hands (WIYH), a large-scale ego-centric dataset comprising over 1,000 hours of ego manipulation data collected in the wild with millimeter-scale motion accuracy. WIYH is built around three components: (1) Oracle Suite, a wearable data-collection kit with an auto-labeling and validation pipeline for accurate motion capture; (2) the WIYH Dataset, which contains diverse multimodal manipulation demonstrations across hundreds of skills and real-world scenarios; and (3) comprehensive annotations, including calibrated visual observations, 3D hand and wrist trajectories, depth, masks, task and sub-task language, and vision-language supervision. We evaluate WIYH through a preliminary real-robot co-training study and a main pre-training study in RoboCasa and on real robots. The results show that ego-centric data improves manipulation policies when robot data are accurate but narrow and that verified WIYH annotations provide substantially stronger pretraining signals than unverified ego data, even when the latter contains more samples. WIYH provides a foundation for studying scalable ego-centric data collection and annotation-quality-aware robot policy learning. All data and documents will be open-sourced.
