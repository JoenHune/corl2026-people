本届研究与论文

Multisensory Continual Learning: Adapting Pretrained Visuomotor Policies to Force

Poster · 首位署名

该篇个人署名单位：Stanford University

摘要原文

Robot manipulation often depends on sensory data beyond vision, especially in contact-rich tasks where force, tactile, or audio feedback reveals interaction states not directly visible from images. Yet such modalities are hardware- and task-specific, and large multisensory datasets remain scarce, making it impractical to pretrain policies with every sensor they may encounter. We study multi-sensory continual learning: adapting a pretrained robot policy to new tasks with newly introduced modalities while preserving performance under the original sensor suite. We propose MuSe, which integrates limited multisensory data into pretrained vision-action policies using token-level multi-stage fusion, multisensory future prediction, and episodic recall on pretraining data. We instantiate MuSe by adding force-torque sensing to a pretrained vision-only policy and evaluate it on real-world manipulation tasks. Experiments show that MuSe not only performs well on contact-rich finetuning tasks but also yields performance gains on pretraining tasks - suggesting that a modest multisensory dataset can enhance general model capability beyond the finetuning distribution.
