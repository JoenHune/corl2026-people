本届研究与论文

Discovering Control Symmetries via Transition Manifold Tangency

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Equivariant architectures improve sample efficiency and generalization in robot learning, but they require the symmetry group to be specified in advance. Existing symmetry-discovery methods focus on static datasets, autonomous dynamical systems, or input--output mappings without separate action representations, and to our knowledge none directly addresses controlled transition datasets $\{(s_i,a_i,s_i')\}$, where actions transform under their own representation and data distribution introduced by the behaviour policy need not be symmetric. We introduce \emph{Transition-Manifold Tangency} (TMT), a geometric framework for discovering symmetries from transition data. Valid transitions form a submanifold of state--action--next-state space, and a dynamical symmetry must move along it: every infinitesimal generator is tangent to this manifold. The estimation target -- the local normal frame -- depends on data geometry but not on the density along the manifold, which yields robustness to nonlinear dynamics and to asymmetric behaviour policies. We provide a model-free local-PCA estimator (TMT-KNN) and a denoiser-based estimator (TMT-NN), and extend both to discrete symmetry discovery via a manifold-consistency test. On continuous benchmarks TMT-NN recovers the ground-truth generator subspace where Jacobian-based and distribution-matching baselines fail under nonlinear dynamics and policy bias respectively. On discrete benchmarks, TMT-NN's candidates approach the denoiser noise floor in absolute manifold distance while a ratio-based metric favours the near-identity candidates of distribution-matching baselines.
