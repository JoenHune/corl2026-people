DexRoam: Enabling Mobile Dexterous Manipulation from Egocentric Human Demonstrations

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University

摘要原文

Mobile dexterous manipulation requires long-horizon coordination of a mobile base, dual arms, and multi-fingered hands, making policy learning highly data-intensive. While human demonstrations offer scalable and diverse interaction data, directly using them for robot learning is challenging due to the embodiment gap. We present DexRoam, a full-stack system for learning mobile dexterous manipulation from tracker-free egocentric human demonstrations. DexRoam uses a consumer VR headset and a head-mounted stereo camera to capture whole-body motion and first-person observations in everyday environments. To transfer these demonstrations, DexRoam introduces a novel whole-body retargeting pipeline that maps human motion into robot-executable base, arm, head, and dexterous hand commands. We train a vision-language-action policy on retargeted human demonstrations and teleoperated robot data, and evaluate how different human-to-robot data ratios affect policy performance under co-training and pretraining-finetuning strategies. Across four real-world tasks, DexRoam improves average success from 33.8% to 71.3% over robot-only training, with consistent gains across reaching, grasping, and mobile coordination. Our analysis further shows that human demonstrations do not replace robot data but substantially improve its data efficiency. We will open-source the full system.

SafeDojo: Safe Reinforcement Learning for VLA via Interactive World Model

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University

摘要原文

Safe control is a prerequisite for real-world embodied intelligence, for which safe reinforcement learning has emerged as a promising paradigm. However, existing safe reinforcement learning methods either require costly real-world exploration or depend on hand-crafted safety functions. Neither scales to vision-language-action models deployed in open-world physical environments. We propose SafeDojo, the first model-based safe reinforcement learning framework for vision-language-action policies designed to learn safe actions through world model-based imagination. Specifically, SafeDojo performs online reinforcement learning on top of an interactive video world model. The world model generates action-conditioned future predictions, from which a tailored ResNet success classifier estimates per-step task progress from imagined frames and a lightweight safety head predicts per-step contact probability from latent context together with the proposed action chunk, enabling simultaneous assessment of task execution and trajectory safety. The decoupled task-reward and safety-cost signals are balanced through a Lagrangian-based constrained GRPO objective, enabling coordinated improvement of task success and safety under explicit constraints. Experiments on SafeLIBERO and real-world Franka deployment demonstrate that SafeDojo consistently outperforms inference-time safety, model-free RL, and model-based RL baselines on five key metrics, achieving over 7% average safe-success improvement across 21 tasks. Our results position world model-based safe reinforcement learning as a scalable and generalizable path toward safe embodied intelligence.
