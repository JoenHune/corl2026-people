本届研究与论文

FocusVLA: Future-Intent Guided Dynamic View Routing for Vision-Language-Action Models

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences / University of Chinese Academy of Sciences / Nanjing Institute of Software Technology

摘要原文

Vision-language-action (VLA) models have become an important paradigm for robotic manipulation by unifying visual perception, language understanding, and action generation. Since reliable manipulation requires sufficient observation of objects, end-effectors, and their spatial relationships, multi-view inputs are widely used to provide complementary spatial information. However, our experiments show that existing VLA models do not fully exploit such multi-view complementarity. The policy tends to persistently rely on the wrist view, lacking the ability to dynamically adjust global-local view dependency across manipulation stages, which limits stage adaptability and robustness. To address this issue, we propose FocusVLA, a stage-aware multi-view routing framework. FocusVLA predicts future interaction intent from historical actions and uses it to guide dynamic view routing during action generation. By adaptively coordinating global and local observations across different task stages, FocusVLA improves multi-view utilization. On RoboTwin 2.0, FocusVLA improves the average success rate over the strongest baseline π0.5 by 10.08%. In real-world evaluation, FocusVLA improves the success rate from 71.7% to 85.0%, achieving a 13.3% gain over π0.5 . Moreover, the intent prediction and dynamic routing modules of FocusVLA can be integrated into existing VLA frameworks without modifying their core architectures.
