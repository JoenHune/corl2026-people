本届研究与论文

ViTaForce: Pairwise Visuo--Tactile--Force Representation Learning for Contact-Rich Imitation Learning

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

Humans excel at contact-rich manipulation by combining visual context, fingertip tactile feedback, and force-sensitive wrist interaction. While robots can access analogous signals, multimodal Diffusion Policies often fuse them as direct conditioning features, without preserving distinct roles or pairwise relations. We present ViTaForce, a cross-modal representation learning framework that learns three paired encoders from RGB vision (scene and hand-eye), dual side tactile pressure maps (T), and high-rate end-effector wrench windows (F). Each encoder is anchored to a distinct physical question: the V–T encoder learns where contact occurs on the fingers (hand-eye vision), the V–F encoder learns when contact will spike from scene approach cues, and the T–F encoder learns what wrench components reflect genuine contact rather than motion-induced bias (tactile-gated force). Validated on assembly tasks such as nut-bolt insertion, these representations enable more efficient policy learning from multimodal feedback and improve accuracy and robustness under external disturbances.
