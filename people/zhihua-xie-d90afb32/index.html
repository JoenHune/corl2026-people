RAMT-VLA: Reliability-Aware Memory Tracing for Historical Support Assessment in Vision-Language-Action Models

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Vision-language-action (VLA) policies are effective generalist robot controllers, but in long-horizon tasks a visually plausible action can be inappropriate unless earlier subgoals have been completed. We introduce historical support, a reliability criterion asking whether task history justifies a candidate action at the current decision step. Based on this criterion, RAMT-VLA keeps the base VLA frozen and adds a lightweight post-policy assessor that scores candidate action chunks, outputs sparse evidence masks for each candidate, and reranks candidates by historical support. The assessor is trained without dense evidence annotations using wrong-history and missing-evidence counterfactuals, with sparsity and faithfulness regularization. With a frozen 0.5B base model, RAMT-VLA achieves 98.1\% average success on LIBERO, 83.3\% on SimplerEnv-WidowX, and 84.0\% on real-world long-horizon tasks. Matched LIBERO ablations, candidate-selection controls, and transfer evaluations show that support-based reranking improves the frozen base policy, with the largest history-dependent gains on long-horizon tasks, beyond generic sampling or policy-confidence reranking.
