本届研究与论文

What Do You Need for Compositional Generalization in Diffusion Planning?

Poster · 首位署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of Toronto

摘要原文

In policy learning, stitching and compositional generalization refer to the extent to which the policy is able to piece together sub-trajectories of data it is trained on to generate new and diverse behaviours. While stitching is a strength of offline reinforcement learning, recent generative behavioural cloning (BC) methods have also shown proficiency at stitching. Yet, the main factors behind this are poorly understood, hindering the development of new algorithms that can reliably stitch by design. Focusing on diffusion planners trained purely via generative BC, we find two properties are key enablers of composition: shift equivariance and local receptive fields. We use these properties to explain data augmentation, data scaling, and inference choices in existing generative BC methods based on diffusion planning. We use our findings to develop Eq-Net, a new architecture for diffusion planners that enables compositionality while being trained and evaluated like a vanilla diffusion planner. We show that Eq-Net both produces much more diverse trajectories than existing diffusion backbones and can be trained on only short sequences while still generating consistent long-horizon plans.
