本届研究与论文

MaD-PF: Modular and Differentiable Particle Filter for Cross-Environment SE(3) LiDAR Localization

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Multi-modal robust localization requires estimating uncertainty over multiple pose hypotheses while adapting perception and motion models across environments and robotic platforms. Classical particle filters provide a principled Bayesian framework for this problem, but their manually specified models and discrete resampling operations limit integration into end-to-end trainable robotic systems, while learning-based localization approaches typically lack the modularity and generalizability. Differentiable particle filters promise to combine these two worlds by replacing filtering components with differentiable alternatives, but existing methods remain confined to either planar state spaces, visual or simulated observations, or single-platform evaluation. We present the first end-to-end differentiable particle filter capable of real-world cross-environment SE(3) LiDAR localization by using a unit dual quaternion formulation, 3D-LiDAR observations, and differentiable motion, observation, and resampling modules. Within this framework, we thoroughly compare different submodules and propose improvements that demonstrate increased robustness on held-out datasets and, in parallel, \qty{89}{\percent} latency reduction for the observation model. We evaluate the framework on real-world LiDAR data, demonstrating zero-shot RMSE of \qty{0.398}{m} or better in unknown environments, on different robotic platforms, or both, and we outperform SotA learning-based localization systems requiring $2.5 \times$ less training time.
