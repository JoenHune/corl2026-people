本届研究与论文

Koopman DCM: Unstable Eigenfunctions as Data-driven Representations for Legged Balancing

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

In legged locomotion, divergent components of motion (DCMs) have emerged as characteristic states for balance control. They isolate the unstable mode of the dynamics, but only apply to reduced models such as the linear inverted pendulum. In this study, we show how divergent components of motion can be more generally cast as Koopman eigenfunctions, and derived from closed-loop data without committing to a kinematic model. Whereas Koopman analysis typically targets small eigenvalues that characterize conserved quantities and persistent dynamics, we deliberately search for unstable eigenpairs. The resulting Koopman DCMs are data-driven observables encoded from measurement histories (signals available on the real robot, requiring neither simulation groundtruth nor full-state estimation) and regularized by their coupling with control inputs through the DCM recursion. Applied to legged balancing, we show how Koopman DCMs learned from one hour of robot data improve tracking of reference walking patterns. We further discuss how Koopman DCMs apply to learning state-based viability constraints when combined with model predictive control.
