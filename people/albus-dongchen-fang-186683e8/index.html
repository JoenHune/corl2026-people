本届研究与论文

Uncertainty-Aware Neural Time Fields for Safe Online Motion Planning in Unknown Environments

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

Safe motion planning during online mapping of unknown environments is critical for autonomous systems operating under limited and noisy perception. Neural Time Fields model arrival time as a learned continuous cost-to-go for motion planning, but existing formulations are typically deterministic and do not account for sensing uncertainty. In particular, Active Neural Time Fields lack mechanisms to ensure safety during online deployment, where limited and noisy observations can degrade the safety of planned trajectories and cause collisions. In this work, we introduce an uncertainty-aware neural time field formulation that models and propagates sensor-induced uncertainty in robot–obstacle distance into arrival-time computation via a risk-sensitive representation. We integrate this formulation into a real-time, closed-loop motion planning framework with chance-constrained trajectory optimization. Experimental results demonstrate reduced collision rates during online navigation while preserving path efficiency compared to deterministic and inflation-based baselines, with additional results demonstrating applicability to manipulator systems.
