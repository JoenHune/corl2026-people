本届研究与论文

Barrier-Constrained Guidance For Real-time Asynchronous VLA Execution

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Renmin University of China

摘要原文

While asynchronous execution enables VLA models to operate in real-time environments by predicting the next chunk during execution, it inevitably introduces boundary misalignment with the committed action stream. Existing delay-aware solutions address this at the prohibitive cost of extensive retraining, architectural modifications, or fixed-prefix inpainting that inflates computation when low latency is vital. To address these problems, we propose FreeBar, a training-free real-time inference plugin that enables robust asynchronous execution with only marginal additional inference cost by casting chunk alignment in flow-based VLAs as barrier-guided action-space constraint. Specifically, FreeBar introduces a lightweight Control Barrier Function (CBF)-inspired consistency constraint between consecutive action chunks and solves the resulting correction in closed form, avoiding costly backpropagation through the VLA action head. By progressively tightening the consistency bound during generation, FreeBar aligns generated chunks with the committed execution stream while preserving early-stage generative flexibility and long-horizon adaptability. Across Kinetix, RoboCasa, and diverse real-world robotic platforms with multiple VLA backbones, FreeBar improves success rates over naive asynchronous execution by 10%--15% on average, with larger gains in highly dynamic scenarios. It also preserves real-time computational efficiency, decreasing inference latency per action chunk over 45% than RTC. Additionally, as a universal plugin, FreeBar remains robust across distinct latency environments, base policies, and hyperparameter settings.
