本届研究与论文

UniFlow: A Unified Multi-Modal Policy for Robot Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Visuomotor manipulation policies face a persistent trade-off: image-only policies inherit the semantic priors of large-scale 2D pretraining but are brittle to lighting, texture, and viewpoint shift, whereas 3D-only policies are robust to such factors yet are often trained from scratch. Some recent multi-modal policies combine both streams but typically process 3D through a from-scratch encoder, preserving this asymmetry. We present UniFlow, a unified 2D+3D co-training recipe for manipulation in which 3D observations are represented as image-shaped pointmaps so that pretrained 2D backbones can be used to encode both modalities, and RGB and pointmap streams are fused by a unified diffusion transformer with per-modality modulation. On average across thirteen dexterous manipulation tasks from the challenging LBM simulation benchmark, UniFlow outperforms a strong RGB-only baseline in a distribution-shifted setting. UniFlow also outperforms the generalist VLA $\pi_{0.5}$ when averaged over five RoboTwin bimanual tasks as well as four real-world YAM tasks.
