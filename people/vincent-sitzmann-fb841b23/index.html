本届研究与论文

Large Video Planner Enables Generalizable Robot Control

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Massachusetts Institute of Technology

摘要原文

General-purpose robots require decision-making models that can generalize across diverse tasks and environments. Recent work has explored building robot foundation models by extending multimodal large language models (MLLMs) with action outputs, forming vision-language-action (VLA) systems. These approaches are motivated by the intuition that the large-scale pretraining of MLLMs on language and images can transfer effectively to robotic perception and instruction understanding. In this work, we explore an alternative paradigm: using large-scale video pretraining as the primary modality for robot foundation models. Unlike static images and language, videos capture spatio-temporal state-action dynamics in the physical world, making them naturally aligned with robotic behavior. To investigate this direction, we curate an internet-scale video dataset of human activities and task demonstrations and train, for the first time at foundation-model scale, an open video model for generative robotic planning. The model generates zero-shot video plans for novel scenes and tasks, which are further processed into executable robot actions. We evaluate task-level generalization through third-party selected tasks in the wild as well as real-robot experiments, demonstrating successful physical execution. Our results show strong instruction following, robust generalization, and real-world feasibility. We release both the model and dataset to support open and reproducible research in video-based robot learning. Our project website with video results is available in the supplementary materials.
