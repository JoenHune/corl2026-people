When One Step Is Enough: An Empirical Study of Euler Integration in Flow-Matching Policies

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Araya Inc.

摘要原文

Flow matching is now the dominant approach for vision-language action (VLA) robot policies, thanks to its expressiveness and simple training objective. However, inference for standard flow matching requires iteratively solving an ODE, limiting the control frequency at deployment. Whilst several works have proposed novel methods to allow for single-shot action generation, we note that the prevailing VLA training recipe already relies on rectified flow matching, which makes one-step and multi-step integration equivalent at the global optimum. We show that across six flow-matching VLAs, one-step action generation largely matches multi-step sampling at scale: the multi-step refinement that these models are typically evaluated with confers little to no consistent advantage at inference. As supported by the theory, this holds as long as models are sufficiently trained, with enough capacity. Practically, the implication is that for large VLAs, the single-step policy comes essentially for free from the multi-step model, enabling low-latency deployment without retraining, distillation, or auxiliary objectives.
