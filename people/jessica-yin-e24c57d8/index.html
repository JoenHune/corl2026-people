本届研究与论文

ADEPT: Accelerating Dexterity via Pre-Training and Post-Training using Reinforcement Learning

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

We introduce Accelerating DExterity via Pre-Training (ADEPT), a framework for learning high degree-of-freedom (DoF) arm-hand dexterity through large-scale RL. ADEPT pretrains a dexterous manipulation policy on a generic object reposing task, then post-trains downstream policies with this pretrained behavior as a prior on contact-rich downstream tasks. This enables learning new behaviors that are otherwise difficult to discover from scratch on multi-fingered robots. We find that the pretrained policy generalizes and can solve the reposing phase of downstream tasks zero-shot. However, naive RL fine-tuning rapidly degrades this capability during task transfer. To address this, we propose a post-training framework that combines behavior-cloning distillation to adapt the actor to new observations, frozen-actor critic warm-up to calibrate it on the downstream reward, and conservative on-policy updates to prevent large policy drift. To safely exploit the full kinematic dexterity of an arm-hand robot, we introduce a joint configuration-space Geometric Fabric controller that mediates between the RL policy and the robot. Finally, we distill the post-trained teacher into a stereo-RGB student policy that zero-shot sim-to-real transfers on a 23 DoF Kuka-Allegro robot. The resulting system solves contact-rich, long-horizon manipulation tasks requiring grasping, reorienting, and insertion across a wide range of initial states.
