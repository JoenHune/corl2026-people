PIGEON: VLM-Driven Object Navigation via Points of Interest Selection

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence / Peking University

摘要原文

Object navigation in unseen indoor environments requires agents to perform semantic search under partial observability. Vision-language models (VLMs) provide strong semantic-spatial priors for this task, but how to interface them with robot navigation remains challenging: dense VLM inference is expensive, while abstracting environments into symbolic memories often separates high-level reasoning from the raw visual evidence that supports it. We propose we propose PIGEON (Point of Interest Guided Exploration for Object Navigation), a VLM-driven framework that formulates object navigation as raw-observation-grounded sparse decision problem. PIGEON introduces Points of Interest (PoIs) as sparse visual decision units that couple geometrically executable waypoints with raw egocentric observations. Rather than using VLMs as dense controllers or restricting them to frontier ranking, PIGEON enables VLMs to select among task-critical PoIs, including exploration frontiers, suspected target objects, traversable stairs, and floor-level summaries, while low-level planners execute continuous motion between them. This PoI interface further makes high-level navigation decisions verifiable, allowing us to develop an RLVR pipeline that improves local VLMs without manual Chain-of-Thought annotations. Extensive experiments on Habitat ObjectNav benchmarks show that PIGEON achieves state-of-the-art zero-shot performance, scales consistently with foundation model capacity, and transfers to Active Embodied Question Answering with only prompt modifications. Real-world deployments on physical robots further demonstrate its robustness and efficiency.

DexRoam: Enabling Mobile Dexterous Manipulation from Egocentric Human Demonstrations

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University

摘要原文

Mobile dexterous manipulation requires long-horizon coordination of a mobile base, dual arms, and multi-fingered hands, making policy learning highly data-intensive. While human demonstrations offer scalable and diverse interaction data, directly using them for robot learning is challenging due to the embodiment gap. We present DexRoam, a full-stack system for learning mobile dexterous manipulation from tracker-free egocentric human demonstrations. DexRoam uses a consumer VR headset and a head-mounted stereo camera to capture whole-body motion and first-person observations in everyday environments. To transfer these demonstrations, DexRoam introduces a novel whole-body retargeting pipeline that maps human motion into robot-executable base, arm, head, and dexterous hand commands. We train a vision-language-action policy on retargeted human demonstrations and teleoperated robot data, and evaluate how different human-to-robot data ratios affect policy performance under co-training and pretraining-finetuning strategies. Across four real-world tasks, DexRoam improves average success from 33.8% to 71.3% over robot-only training, with consistent gains across reaching, grasping, and mobile coordination. Our analysis further shows that human demonstrations do not replace robot data but substantially improve its data efficiency. We will open-source the full system.

DeCAL: Towards Physically-Grounded Dexterous Vision-Language-Action Models via Contact-Aware Latent Co-Imagination

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University / Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence

摘要原文

Dexterous manipulation involves contact-rich and fine-grained interactions with the physical world, posing significant challenges for existing vision-language-action (VLA) models due to severe visual occlusions and complex contact dynamics. While recent works have incorporated tactile sensing into robotic manipulation, most approaches still rely on homogeneous multimodal fusion, lacking adaptive tactile integration and explicit modeling of physical dynamics. In this work, we present DeCAL, a physically-grounded dexterous vision-language-action model that unifies understanding, imagination and action generation for contact-rich dexterous manipulation. Built upon a Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) architecture, DeCAL leverages specialized experts for each capability while enabling efficient information flow among them. To effectively leverage tactile information, we introduce Adaptive Visuo-Tactile Fusion that dynamically regulates tactile interactions via a contact-aware gating strategy. Furthermore, we propose Visuo-Tactile Latent Co-Imagination to jointly model visual and tactile dynamics, equipping the policy with implicit physical world knowledge. Experimental results show that DeCAL consistently achieves state-of-the-art performance across all tasks, attaining a 71% average success rate and an 83.4% progress success rate, while also demonstrating strong generalization to unseen scenarios.

SafeDojo: Safe Reinforcement Learning for VLA via Interactive World Model

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University

摘要原文

Safe control is a prerequisite for real-world embodied intelligence, for which safe reinforcement learning has emerged as a promising paradigm. However, existing safe reinforcement learning methods either require costly real-world exploration or depend on hand-crafted safety functions. Neither scales to vision-language-action models deployed in open-world physical environments. We propose SafeDojo, the first model-based safe reinforcement learning framework for vision-language-action policies designed to learn safe actions through world model-based imagination. Specifically, SafeDojo performs online reinforcement learning on top of an interactive video world model. The world model generates action-conditioned future predictions, from which a tailored ResNet success classifier estimates per-step task progress from imagined frames and a lightweight safety head predicts per-step contact probability from latent context together with the proposed action chunk, enabling simultaneous assessment of task execution and trajectory safety. The decoupled task-reward and safety-cost signals are balanced through a Lagrangian-based constrained GRPO objective, enabling coordinated improvement of task success and safety under explicit constraints. Experiments on SafeLIBERO and real-world Franka deployment demonstrate that SafeDojo consistently outperforms inference-time safety, model-free RL, and model-based RL baselines on five key metrics, achieving over 7% average safe-success improvement across 21 tasks. Our results position world model-based safe reinforcement learning as a scalable and generalizable path toward safe embodied intelligence.

LiMA: Bridging Long-term Imagination to Real-time Dexterous Manipulation via Asynchronous Diffusion

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University / Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence

摘要原文

Effective dexterous manipulation demands a seamless integration of long-term temporal foresight and rapid reactive control. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, while proficient in high-level reasoning, often lack a fine-grained understanding of physical dynamics and spatial perception. Conversely, World-Action Models (WAMs) typically suffer from high inference latency due to iterative generation. These deficiencies result in a critical temporal misalignment where the model's intent fails to adapt to rapid physical contact changes. To overcome this fundamental bottleneck, we propose LiMA, an asynchronous dual-system generative framework that systematically decouples intent planning from reactive execution. LiMA organizes computation into a multi-scale hierarchy: a slow system handles sparse long-horizon spatiotemporal intent generation, while a fast system focuses on dense high-frequency motion refinement. To align sparse intent predictions with dense action trajectories, we introduce a Latent Schrödinger Bridge Coupling mechanism that formulates refinement as an entropy-regularized probabilistic transport process. LiMA reduces inference latency by 45.8% compared with Cosmos-Policy via asynchronous decoupling. Evaluated across six bimanual dexterous manipulation tasks spanning multiple horizons, LiMA achieves an overall success rate of 70.8% and an average subtask success rate of 78.5%, while maintaining performance in unseen scenarios.

RoboDreamer: Anticipatory Humanoid Locomotion with Predictive State-Space Models

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Humanoid locomotion requires control policies that remain stable under imperfect sensing while capturing long-term temporal dependencies for consistent and adaptive movement. Conventional end-to-end policies based on multi-layer perceptrons lack explicit temporal memory and often struggle to recover from sensor dropouts, whereas Transformer-based policies introduce substantial computational overhead that limits their use in high-frequency real-time control. To address these challenges, we propose RoboDreamer, a framework built upon Mamba's selective state-space mechanism for efficient long-horizon temporal modeling. Through randomized temporal masking during training, RoboDreamer learns to recover missing observations and predict future states, which supports robust control under partial sensory corruption. Leveraging this capability, RoboDreamer further enables action chunking, generating multi-step actions within a single inference pass to improve control smoothness. Experiments show that RoboDreamer outperforms MLP and Transformer baselines in robustness and efficiency, maintaining stable locomotion under masked observations while supporting real-time deployment for high-frequency humanoid control.

HAF-VLA: Hierarchical Action Flow Framework for Humanoid Vision-Language-Action Control

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University

摘要原文

Humanoid robots hold great promise as general-purpose agents in human-centered environments, yet adapting generalist vision-language-action (VLA) policies to real-world humanoid loco-manipulation remains challenging. Unlike fixed-base or wheeled manipulators, humanoids must coordinate locomotion, posture, gaze, and dual-arm manipulation over a high-dimensional whole-body action space, where inaccurate base or waist motions can easily induce unstable and aggressive upper-body compensation. We propose HAF-VLA, a hierarchical action-flow framework for humanoid VLA control. Built upon a pretrained flow-matching VLA, HAF-VLA inherits the vision-language prefix encoder and action expert weights, and adapts them to humanoid whole-body control with learnable stage embeddings and cross-stage KV-cache conditioning. Instead of generating the full action chunk in a single pass, HAF-VLA progressively denoises a shared whole-body action space: first locomotion and head commands, then waist refinement, and finally full-body manipulation. To preserve structural dependencies across stages, we introduce a cross-stage KV cache conditioning mechanism, enabling subsequent action generation stages to refine and augment their outputs based on the action representations from preceding stages. Experiment results across six real-world tasks demonstrate that our framework significantly enhances the performance of existing models in humanoid loco-manipulation tasks, demonstrating an effective pathway for adapting strong mobile-manipulation VLAs to humanoid whole-body control.
