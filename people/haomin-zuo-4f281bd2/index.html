本届研究与论文

PAWN: Preference-Aware Semantic NFT for Online Reinforcement Learning of Flow-based VLAs

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

Online reinforcement learning for flow-based manipulation policies is challenging because multi-step solver-based action generation makes the action likelihoods required by standard policy-ratio objectives difficult to access. Hopefully, NFT-style methods offer a promising likelihood-free route for local policy optimization, and recent embodied extensions have begun to adapt this idea to flow-based robot policies. However, several structural issues remain: trajectory-level outcomes provide noisy and misaligned credit for action chunks and their intermediate solver states; full-space mirrored comparisons ignore the compositional geometry of robot actions; and local updates may still follow noisy behavior-sampled continuations without explicitly identifying better alternatives, which may be guided toward the manifold of suboptimal actions. We present Preference-Aware Semantic NFT (PAWN), a unified NFT-style online RL framework that addresses these issues through structured local preference optimization. PAWN derives implicit chunk-level preference signals by lifting solver-step density comparisons to chunk-path scores, reorganizes mirrored local evidence into SE(3)-aware motion modes, and uses conditional pair guidance to favor locally better continuations. Experiments on flow-based manipulation benchmarks show consistent gains in adaptation efficiency, final success, and OOD robustness over strong online RL baselines.
