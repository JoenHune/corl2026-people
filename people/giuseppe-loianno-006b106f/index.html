本届研究与论文

HyperDrone: Simulation-to-Reality Transfer at Millions of Frames per Second

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of California, Berkeley

摘要原文

We present HyperDrone, an extremely fast quadrotor simulator that is focused on high-performance batch rendering and is more than 20x faster than existing state-of-the-art batch quadrotor simulators. We design a ray-tracing engine that can take full advantage of modern ray-tracing hardware (ray-tracing cores) and render RGB and depth observations. HyperDrone supports configurable levels of fidelity, including reflections, realistic lighting, motion blur, anti-aliasing, and Physically Based Rendering (PBR). Even in the highest-fidelity settings, HyperDrone achieves millions of Frames Per Second (FPS) on consumer-grade GPUs, while simulating realistic 3D scenes. We demonstrate the merits of HyperDrone by training a vision-based yaw-control policy purely in simulation and transferring it to a real small-scale quadrotor (Crazyflie) with a camera, running completely onboard.
