本届研究与论文

RoboGene: Boosting VLA Pre-training via Diversity-Driven Agentic Framework for Real-World Task Generation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University

摘要原文

The pursuit of general-purpose robotic manipulation is hindered by the scarcity of diverse, real-world interaction data. Unlike the large-scale web vision-language data, robotic data collection is an active process incurring prohibitive physical costs. As a result, the key challenge is not merely to collect more robot data, but to obtain training data that provides informative learning knowledge by broadening behavioral coverage and capturing meaningful variations in objects, scenes, and skills. However, how to automatically curate task proposals that maximize the utility of collected robot data remains a critical yet under-explored challenge. To address this, we introduce RoboGene, an agentic framework designed to automate the generation of diverse and informative manipulation tasks across heterogeneous robotic embodiments, spanning single-arm, dual-arm, and mobile robots. RoboGene integrates three core components: (1) diversity-driven sampling for broad task coverage, (2) agentic self-reflection for task-quality assessment, and (3) human-verified feedback memory for execution-grounded validation and continual refinement. We conduct extensive quantitative analysis and large-scale real-world experiments, collecting datasets of 18k trajectories and introducing novel metrics to assess task quality in terms of feasibility and diversity. Results demonstrate that RoboGene significantly outperforms frontier foundation models (e.g., GPT-4o, Gemini 2.5 Pro). Furthermore, real-world experiments show that VLA models trained on data generated by RoboGene achieve higher success rates and stronger generalization, highlighting the importance of high-quality task generation. Our project is available at https://x-robogene.github.io/.
