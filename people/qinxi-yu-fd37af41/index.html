本届研究与论文

Box Trajectories as Compact State Representations for World Action Models

Poster · 首位署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

摘要原文

World action models (WAMs) are a category of policy models that explicitly predict both actions and future world states. They have been shown to outperform action-only models across various robotic manipulation tasks. However, representing the future with full-frame video prediction can be computationally expensive during both training and inference. This raises a central question: what is the minimal state representation that preserves the planning and supervision benefits of future prediction? In this paper, we propose BoxWAM, which replace dense future frames with oriented bounding box trajectories of task-relevant objects. By explicitly imagining object-centric future motion, our method provides a lightweight prediction target that captures the key physical changes induced by robot actions. We show that box trajectory prediction substantially reduces computational cost while improving action prediction performance, suggesting that object-centric compact states can serve as an efficient alternative to video-based world action modeling for robotic manipulation.
