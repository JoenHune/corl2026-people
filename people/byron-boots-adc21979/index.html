A Balanced Data Diet: Addressing the Bottleneck in Mega-Scale RL for Robot Control

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of Washington

摘要原文

General-purpose robots must perform a wide range of tasks from agile locomotion to dexterous manipulation, but current sim2real RL pipelines depend on per-task structural priors to train such as shaped rewards and demonstrations. Recent work has shown that diverse simulator resets, combined with massively parallel simulation, can replace much of this engineering. Yet, uniformly sampling over this distribution wastes a growing fraction of environments on task configurations the policy has already mastered or cannot yet attempt, so returns flatten when scaling the number of parallel environments. We introduce a simple adaptive sampler, Success Guided Sampling (SGS), that tracks the policy's frontier of capability, concentrating experience on task configurations where success rate sits in a productive band. This results in continued performance gains up to 1M+ parallel environments, 16× beyond prior work, under simple reward structures and no demonstrations. We demonstrate this on two settings where prior approaches required per-task engineering. For agile locomotion, a single policy acquires multiple long-horizon skills that previously demanded per-task priors and separate training runs. For dexterous manipulation, we present the first fully-RL solutions to the nut-and-bolt and rod assembly tasks from the NIST board.

Online World Modeling Enables Real-World Inverse Reinforcement Learning from Observation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of Washington

摘要原文

Current methods in robot learning are fundamentally bottlenecked by one or more of: hand-designed rewards, simulation modeling, or action supervision (e.g. teleoperation) each requiring significant domain expertise, engineering effort, and robot-operator labor. Towards eliminating these bottlenecks, this work pursues observational learning via Inverse Reinforcement Learning from Observation (IRLfO) in which only access to task observations (e.g. video) is assumed. Due to the challenging setting and limitations of RL methods, IRLfO has thus far remained impractical for real-world robot learning. Here, we present the first IRL method to learn visual manipulation in the real world from scratch, and the first real-world demonstration of positive online transfer across visual manipulation tasks from scratch. In under 40 minutes, MPAIL2 learns pick-and-place from scratch to 82% success, where RL and BC with equal interaction and demonstration budgets reach only 0% and 12% despite their reward and action supervision.
