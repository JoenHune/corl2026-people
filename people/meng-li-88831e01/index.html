RoboGene: Boosting VLA Pre-training via Diversity-Driven Agentic Framework for Real-World Task Generation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics

摘要原文

The pursuit of general-purpose robotic manipulation is hindered by the scarcity of diverse, real-world interaction data. Unlike the large-scale web vision-language data, robotic data collection is an active process incurring prohibitive physical costs. As a result, the key challenge is not merely to collect more robot data, but to obtain training data that provides informative learning knowledge by broadening behavioral coverage and capturing meaningful variations in objects, scenes, and skills. However, how to automatically curate task proposals that maximize the utility of collected robot data remains a critical yet under-explored challenge. To address this, we introduce RoboGene, an agentic framework designed to automate the generation of diverse and informative manipulation tasks across heterogeneous robotic embodiments, spanning single-arm, dual-arm, and mobile robots. RoboGene integrates three core components: (1) diversity-driven sampling for broad task coverage, (2) agentic self-reflection for task-quality assessment, and (3) human-verified feedback memory for execution-grounded validation and continual refinement. We conduct extensive quantitative analysis and large-scale real-world experiments, collecting datasets of 18k trajectories and introducing novel metrics to assess task quality in terms of feasibility and diversity. Results demonstrate that RoboGene significantly outperforms frontier foundation models (e.g., GPT-4o, Gemini 2.5 Pro). Furthermore, real-world experiments show that VLA models trained on data generated by RoboGene achieve higher success rates and stronger generalization, highlighting the importance of high-quality task generation. Our project is available at https://x-robogene.github.io/.

RoboAug: One Annotation to Hundreds of Scenes via Region-Contrastive Data Augmentation for Robotic Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics

摘要原文

Enhancing the generalization of robotic learning in diverse unseen environments remains a fundamental challenge. Existing approaches often rely on large-scale pretraining, which is labor-intensive and time-consuming, or semantic data augmentation methods that assume flawless upstream object detection in real-world scenarios. In this work, we propose RoboAug, a novel generative data augmentation framework that reduces reliance on large-scale pretraining and perfect visual recognition by requiring only a single image with bounding box annotations for dataset construction. Leveraging this minimal supervision, RoboAug employs pretrained generative models for precise semantic augmentation and introduces a plug-and-play region-contrastive loss to guide attention toward task-relevant regions, thereby enhancing generalization and task success rates. We conduct extensive real-world experiments on three robotic platforms—UR-5e, AgileX, and Tien Kung 2.0—covering over 35k rollouts. Experimental results show that RoboAug consistently outperforms state-of-the-art augmentation baselines. In unseen environments with varying backgrounds, distractors, and lighting conditions, RoboAug improves success rates over the no-augmentation baseline from 0.09 to 0.47 on UR-5e, 0.16 to 0.60 on AgileX, and 0.19 to 0.67 on Tien Kung 2.0. These results demonstrate the superior generalization and effectiveness of RoboAug in real-world manipulation tasks.

HAF-VLA: Hierarchical Action Flow Framework for Humanoid Vision-Language-Action Control

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics

摘要原文

Humanoid robots hold great promise as general-purpose agents in human-centered environments, yet adapting generalist vision-language-action (VLA) policies to real-world humanoid loco-manipulation remains challenging. Unlike fixed-base or wheeled manipulators, humanoids must coordinate locomotion, posture, gaze, and dual-arm manipulation over a high-dimensional whole-body action space, where inaccurate base or waist motions can easily induce unstable and aggressive upper-body compensation. We propose HAF-VLA, a hierarchical action-flow framework for humanoid VLA control. Built upon a pretrained flow-matching VLA, HAF-VLA inherits the vision-language prefix encoder and action expert weights, and adapts them to humanoid whole-body control with learnable stage embeddings and cross-stage KV-cache conditioning. Instead of generating the full action chunk in a single pass, HAF-VLA progressively denoises a shared whole-body action space: first locomotion and head commands, then waist refinement, and finally full-body manipulation. To preserve structural dependencies across stages, we introduce a cross-stage KV cache conditioning mechanism, enabling subsequent action generation stages to refine and augment their outputs based on the action representations from preceding stages. Experiment results across six real-world tasks demonstrate that our framework significantly enhances the performance of existing models in humanoid loco-manipulation tasks, demonstrating an effective pathway for adapting strong mobile-manipulation VLAs to humanoid whole-body control.
