本届研究与论文

RMMBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Robotic Mobile Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Meituan

摘要原文

Although the advancement of vision-language models (VLMs) has endowed robots with enhanced environmental understanding and task reasoning, a comprehensive evaluation methodology is important to advance the integration of VLMs in robotic navigation and manipulation. However, current benchmarks lack comprehensive methods to evaluate diverse robotic tasks, and evaluation metrics remain relatively constrained, making it difficult to assess the embodied capabilities of VLMs in a thorough and fine-grained manner. To address the issues, we propose RMMBench, an evaluation benchmark that requires robots to understand language instructions and perform long-horizon tasks in continuous spaces. RMMBench seamlessly integrates high- and low-level embodied tasks into a unified framework, constructing a ``navigation-manipulation'' task suite comprising 70 core use cases that range from localized manipulation to long-horizon composite navigation. The results reveal that the existing leading VLMs still face major challenges in spatial localization accuracy when coping with mobile manipulation tasks. The findings highlight the necessity of enhancing robots' spatial perception capabilities during long-period interactions to bridge this gap, and emphasize the potential for further research in this field.
