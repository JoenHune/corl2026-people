本届研究与论文

AdvDex: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from Human Demonstrations via Joint-Aligned Actions and Adversarial Learning

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Zhejiang University

摘要原文

Dexterous manipulation remains a fundamental capability for embodied intelligence, yet its scalability is severely bottlenecked by expensive data acquisition and hardware fragmentation. The current ecosystem lacks a unified data paradigm, preventing the aggregation of demonstrations across structurally diverse robotic platforms. Furthermore, shared visuomotor policies often suffer from visual entanglement, where task-critical spatial semantics are confounded with embodiment-specific appearances. To overcome these barriers, we present AdvDex, a unified Vision-Language-Action framework for scalable cross-embodiment dexterous manipulation. First, we introduce OmniShare, a massive multimodal human demonstration dataset that provides high-fidelity priors to bypass teleoperation bottlenecks. Second, we propose the Joint-Aligned Action Space (JAAS), a canonical $SE(3)$ wrist and 15-finger-joint representation that seamlessly harmonizes human hands, dexterous robotic hands, and parallel grippers. Finally, we employ domain-adversarial learning to explicitly decouple embodiment-specific visual features from universal task skills. Extensive physical experiments demonstrate that AdvDex consistently outperforms single-embodiment baselines, enabling robust zero-shot policy transfer and rapid few-shot adaptation across heterogeneous hardware and unseen environments.
