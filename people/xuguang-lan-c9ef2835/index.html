Phase-Conditioned Affordance Fields for SE(3)-Equivariant Visuomotor Policies

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Xi'an Jiaotong University

摘要原文

$SE(3)$-equivariance provides a useful geometric inductive bias for visuomotor policies, encoding that actions covary with rigid transformations of the observation. However, existing $SE(3)$-equivariant visuomotor policies build this bias on a global equivariance assumption that treats the entire observation as governed by a single rigid transformation, which breaks down in real-world manipulation tasks where the task-relevant region changes across different objects. We address this by learning a phase-conditioned affordance field (PAF) over the observed point cloud, a per-point score that the $SE(3)$-equivariant policy uses in two complementary ways. Its score-weighted centroid serves as a learned, phase-conditioned anchor that canonicalizes the observation and the action around the active region, replacing the fixed gripper-frame reference, and the score itself is appended as a per-point feature. This introduces a local geometric inductive bias, while preserving the policy's global $SE(3)$ equivariance. We validate on multiple MimicGen tasks under varying spatial randomization, and PAF consistently improves the success rate of the underlying policy. On multi-object real-robot tasks, PAF outperforms baselines and remains more robust under lighting variations.

VoxAfford: Multi-Scale Voxel-Token Fusion for Open-Vocabulary 3D Affordance Detection

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Open-vocabulary 3D affordance detection requires localizing interaction regions on point clouds given novel affordance descriptions. Recent methods extend multimodal large language models (MLLMs) with special output tokens that are decoded into segmentation masks. However, these tokens are produced through autoregressive generation, which models sequential dependencies rather than spatial neighborhood relations, leaving them semantically rich but spatially impoverished for 3D localization. We propose Voxel-enhanced Affordance detection (VoxAfford), which bypasses this bottleneck by injecting multi-scale geometric features from a frozen pre-trained 3D VQVAE encoder into the output tokens after generation. Each output token uses its affordance semantics as a query to retrieve relevant geometric patterns from its paired voxel scale via cross-attention, with a learned compatibility gate controlling the injection strength. The enhanced tokens are then aggregated into a spatially-aware affordance prompt through semantic-conditioned attention and propagated alongside per-point features to generate the final mask. Experiments on open-vocabulary affordance detection tasks show that VoxAfford achieves state-of-the-art performance with an approximately 10% absolute improvement in instance-level mIoU, and real robot experiments confirm zero-shot transfer to novel objects.

Scaling World Model for Hierarchical Manipulation Policies

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models are promising for generalist robot manipulation but remain brittle in out-of-distribution (OOD) settings, especially with limited real-robot data. To resolve the generalization bottleneck, we introduce a hierarchical Vision-Language-Action framework VISTA that leverages the generalization of large-scale pre-trained world model for robust and generalizable VIsual Subgoal TAsk decomposition (VISTA). Our hierarchical framework VISTA consists of a world model as the high-level planner and a VLA as the low-level executor. The high-level world model first divides manipulation tasks into subtask sequences with goal images, and the low-level policy follows the textual and visual guidance to generate action sequences. Compared to raw textual instructions, these synthesized goal images provide visually and physically grounded details for low-level policies, making it feasible to generalize across unseen objects and novel scenarios. We evaluate both visual goal synthesis and our hierarchical VLA policies in massive out-of-distribution scenarios, and the performance of the same-structured VLA in novel scenarios could boost from 14% to 69% with the guidance generated by the world model. Results demonstrate that our method outperforms previous baselines with a clear margin, particularly in out-of-distribution scenarios.
