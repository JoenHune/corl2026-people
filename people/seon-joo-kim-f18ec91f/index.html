本届研究与论文

HuRo: Robotizing Human Videos for Scalable VLA Pretraining

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Yonsei University

摘要原文

Human video datasets have emerged as a compelling way to alternative to expensive real-robot data, leveraging visual diversity present in human videos. To bridge the inherent human-to-robot embodiment gap, existing methods either constrain video sources to narrow, task-aligned environments or focus solely on visual representation transfer while leaving the underlying action trajectories unutilized. In this work, we focus on robotized video as a means to further capitalize on human data, examining its effectiveness as a scalable source for policy pretraining. To this end, we propose a robotization pipeline capable of handling heterogeneous human videos, effectively accommodating different levels of initial annotation by inferring missing intermediate signals. Building on this pipeline, we construct the HuRo Dataset, a large scale collection containing about 630K robotized episodes and 142M processed frames converted from a wide variety of human video sources. Across four real-world downstream manipulation tasks, we show performance scales with the amount of robotized pretraining data. Policies pretrained on our dataset surpass strong baseline foundation models that were pretrained solely on large scale, purely robotic data. These results suggest that robotized human videos can serve as a practical and scalable source of supervision for robot policy learning.
