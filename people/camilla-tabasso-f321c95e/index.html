本届研究与论文

PolyFusion: Collision-Free Robot Learning using Bernstein Polynomials

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Generative imitation-learning policies predict dense action sequences that deployed systems cannot easily verify, repair, or procedurally generate supervision for. We present PolyFusion, a pipeline that replaces the dense action head with a piecewise Bernstein-polynomial representation parameterized by a small set of control points. A generative policy is used to sample control points conditioned on perceived scene geometry and affordance-predicted grasp poses. The properties of Bernstein polynomials enable us to: (i) procedurally generate synthetic training data from a single demonstration; (ii) analytically guarantee safety; and (iii) repair infeasible trajectories using a sequential convex projection that minimally adjusts control points while preserving task-dependent poses (e.g., start, grasp, and place). We show that PolyFusion results in a compact polynomial parameterization providing ~82-200x faster wall-clock time, uses ~129x fewer FLOPs per episode, and ~10x fewer gradient updates, while still maintaining performance comparable to current state-of-the-art methods.
