本届研究与论文

Self-Supervised Dispersive Sampling for Kinodynamic Search

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

Control-space tree search sidesteps the steering requirement of geometric search methods and offers an asymptotic route to natively feasible kinodynamic trajectories. In practice, however, control-space search fails on the simplest of motion-planning instances for nonlinear systems such as quadrotors. This failure is rooted in poor geometric coverage induced when naive control-sampling measures are pushed forward through system dynamics. We resolve this problem by learning a lightweight sampler whose pushforward achieves low geometric dispersion in the local search space. The central idea is to utilize a well-dispersed reference map as a training scaffold, eliminating the need for an external expert. Our experiments then demonstrate that the learned sampler enables control-space search for quadrotor motion planning when naive sampling measures simply fail. Once enabled, control-space planning is shown to generate highly agile trajectory solutions in an obstacle-dense setting as compared to established trajectory optimization techniques. We also show that the geometric reach of the learned model dominates popular motion primitive libraries, spanning roughly thrice their volume. Arbitrarily low-dispersion primitives are thus made available for any state merely by scaling test-time compute.
