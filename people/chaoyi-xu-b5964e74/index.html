RealDexUMI: A Wearable Universal Manipulation Interface for Dexterous Robot Learning

Poster · 首位署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University / BeingBeyond

摘要原文

Learning dexterous manipulation requires demonstrations that preserve fine hand-object interactions while remaining executable at deployment. Existing pipelines either lose deployable dexterity through retargeting or embodiment conversion, or rely on robot-specific teleoperation that is costly to scale and often lacks intuitive, contact-aware control for dexterous data collection. We present RealDexUMI, a wearable universal manipulation interface built around a shared dexterous end-effector module that integrates a lightweight dexterous hand, in-hand vision, and fingertip tactile sensing. A palm-side isomorphic teleoperation glove maps human finger inputs to robot-hand joint commands, enabling real-time, retargeting-free, intuitive, and precise hand control. The shared hand and sensing modules yield zero-gap end-effector data, with matched in-hand observations, tactile signals, contacts, and hand actions between collection and deployment. Across eight real-robot tasks spanning fine-grained, contact-rich, long-horizon, and bimanual manipulation, policies trained on RealDexUMI data achieve an average success rate of 88.75%, generalize to unseen initial poses, and transfer across three embodiments.

Being-H0.5: Scaling Human-Centric Robot Learning for Cross-Embodiment Generalization

Oral · 共同署名

摘要原文

We present Being-H0.5, a Vision-Language-Action foundation model for cross-embodiment generalization. To address morphological diversity and data scarcity, we propose a human-centric learning paradigm treating human interaction traces as a universal ``mother tongue'' for physical skills. We curate a 35,000-hour embodied pre-training corpus spanning human and 30 robotic embodiments. Central to our approach is a unified action space that maps human hand motion and heterogeneous robot controls into semantically aligned slots, enabling low-resource robots to bootstrap from human and high-resource platforms. Built on this foundation, we develop unified sequence modeling and multi-task pre-training to jointly learn from human demonstrations and robot trajectories. Architecturally, Being-H0.5 extends conventional Mixture-of-Transformers with a novel Mixture-of-Flow, which separates shared motor primitives from embodiment-specific action experts. For real-world stability, we introduce Manifold-Preserving Gating to mitigate sensory shift and Universal Async Chunking for chunked control across varying latencies and control rates. Being-H0.5 achieves state-of-the-art results on LIBERO and RoboCasa, and demonstrates strong cross-embodiment performance across five real robot platforms.

MoDex: Towards Universal Multi-Object Dexterous Grasping

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Peking University / BeingBeyond

摘要原文

Multi-object grasping is a fundamental yet challenging problem, constrained by data collection costs, varying spatial layouts, and the need for broad generalization. To address these challenges, we propose MoDex, a data-efficient framework that provides the first unified solution for universal multi-object dexterous grasping across diverse spatial layouts. MoDex accomplishes this by formulating the task as a single-step decision problem of learning residual corrections to a reference trajectory, and introducing a spatially adaptive dual-strategy that performs single-shot grasping for proximal objects and sequential interaction for separated objects. Extensive evaluations show that MoDex significantly outperforms baselines in proximal configurations, achieving 64.17% success on unseen objects, and uniquely succeeds in separated layouts where prior methods struggle. Further experiments demonstrate its generalization across robot embodiments, scalability and zero-shot sim-to-real deployment through a vision-based policy trained solely on simulation data.
