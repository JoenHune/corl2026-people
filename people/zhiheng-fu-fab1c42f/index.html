本届研究与论文

Geometry-Aware Absolute Pose Regression via Geometric Pose Proxy

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Absolute pose regression (APR) provides an attractive route to real-time camera relocalization through a single feed-forward pass. However, the image-to-pose formulation hides the perspective and scene geometries that make localization well constrained, limiting its accuracy. To address this mismatch, we reformulate APR as a geometry-aware regression pipeline. The query image is first lifted into a scene pointmap, providing the downstream regressor with a geometric representation rather than appearance-only features. We then introduce a Gram-based pose proxy inspired by classical absolute pose estimation, where the network predicts a structured Gram matrix that jointly encodes perspective constraints and scene geometry. During training, we further propose geometry-guided pointmap augmentation, which constructs additional samples at novel viewpoints directly in the pointmap space. Compared with novel-view-synthesis-enhanced APR, it provides more geometry-focused samples without costly rendering or training an additional neural scene representation. Indoor and outdoor experiments show that our method substantially improves accuracy while retaining efficient feed-forward inference, achieving a favorable accuracy-efficiency trade-off.
