本届研究与论文

Object-Centric Conditioning for Visuomotor Flow Matching

Poster · 首位署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of Chinese Academy of Sciences / Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences

摘要原文

Robot visuomotor policies are commonly formulated as autoregressive, diffusion-based, or more recently, flow matching models. Among them, Action-to-Action (A2A) flow matching improves inference efficiency by initializing generation from historical action priors rather than stochastic noise. However, strong dependence on historical motion priors, together with entangled global visual representations, can reduce robustness under spatial out-of-distribution (OOD) shifts and visual distractors. In this work, we propose SlotFlow, an object-centric flow matching policy for robust visuomotor manipulation. SlotFlow decouples scene observations into semantic (“what”) and geometric (“where“) representations to provide object-aware policy conditioning and object-centric spatial grounding. The semantic representation suppresses irrelevant background correlations, while the geometric representation improves adaptation to shifted object configurations under spatial perturbations. Extensive experiments demonstrate that SlotFlow significantly improves robustness under visual distractors and severe spatial perturbations while preserving the low-step inference efficiency advantages of A2A flow matching in both simulation and real-world robotic manipulation tasks.
