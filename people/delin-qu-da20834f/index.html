本届研究与论文

Seek-before-Act: Generative Visual Foresight for Occlusion-Aware Wrist-View Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have shown substantial progress in enabling robots to act with instructions and visual observations, yet most existing policies implicitly assume that the initial observation is completely actionable. This assumption often breaks down in realistic scenes, especially wrist-view settings, where the target object may be partially visible or fully occluded. In such cases, manipulation failures may arise not from insufficient manipulation skill, but from the lack of a task-ready observation. To address this problem, we propose Seek-before-Act, a plug-in active approach framework that enables a robot to acquire right observations before downstream manipulation. Given only a language instruction and an initial wrist RGB image, Seek-before-Act uses task-conditioned visual foresight to imagine target-revealing view sequence, lifts them into an planning-oriented 3D representation, and plans a collision-aware approach motion toward a viewpoint that improves target visibility, reachability, and downstream manipulability. A semantic verification module further checks whether the reached view is ready for manipulation and triggers closed-loop replanning when necessary. Experiments in simulation and real-world setups show that Seek-before-Act substantially improves downstream manipulation success under occlusion, clutter, limited viewpoints, visual noise, and moderate scene changes, while remaining compatible with existing VLA policies and model-based planners.
