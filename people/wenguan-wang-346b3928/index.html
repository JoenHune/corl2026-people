本届研究与论文

Compositional Diffusion Policy for Vision–Language–Action Agents

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) agents face a combinatorial task space where semantic and physical requirements are recombined in novel ways. However, existing holistic policies typically entangle them in a single latent representation, limiting generalization to unseen compositions and compromising physical feasibility. To this end, this work proposes an energy-based compositional diffusion policy that models robot action trajectory generation with distinct semantic and physical energy terms. Specifically, the agent factorizes multimodal inputs into grounded semantic factors and models them as implicit semantic energies. In parallel, it formulates physical constraints, including motion smoothness and kinematic manipulability, as explicit differentiable energies. These energies are jointly combined to guide trajectory generation toward both task satisfaction and physical feasibility. Our agent shows consistent improvements in compositional generalization and physical validity in real-world experiments and across some public benchmarks.
