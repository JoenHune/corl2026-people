Chunk-wise Model-based Policy Optimization

Spotlight · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Shanghai Innovation Institute / Shanghai Jiao Tong University

摘要原文

Model-based reinforcement learning has shown superior sample efficiency by leveraging the learned latent dynamics either in the form of derivative-free planning/imagined rollout training or dynamics gradient-based policy updates. While these approaches achieve comparatively fast learning, they all suffer, to varying degrees, from issues of recurrent compounding errors, high inference-time latency, and instability in backward propagation through time (BPTT). In this work, we propose a model-based RL approach that uses the chunk world model as a differentiable training-time evaluator to optimize the chunk actor without recurrent model invocation. Through the collaborative chunk-wise prediction of the world model and policy, we can generate H-step hypothetical rollouts in a single forward pass and directly optimize value expansion, avoiding the costly and unstable BPTT. We evaluate our method on 19 challenging high-dimensional locomotion tasks from HumanoidBench and deploy our method to the real-world for quadruped bipedal walking. Our code will be made publicly available.

Playing Soccer Like Humans Do: Learning Versatile and Agile Humanoid Soccer via Latent Motion Priors

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Humanoid soccer requires a robot to seamlessly combine agile locomotion, ball manipulation, and impact-rich contact dynamics across a continuous, dynamic behavioral spectrum. Yet existing approaches either cover only a limited set of skills, or forego motion priors entirely and produce unnatural behaviors, leaving the combination of a unified skill set and human-like motion quality unaddressed. We present SoccerBot, a hierarchical reinforcement learning framework that resolves both limitations through a structured latent motion prior distilled from large-scale human soccer motion capture data. By constraining policy learning within this dynamically plausible motion manifold, a single task-conditioned policy naturally learns to perform moving, dribbling, trapping, and kicking in a unified formulation, while inherently producing human-like motion and fluid transitions during commanded skill switching. Augmented by robustness-oriented simulation design, SoccerBot transfers zero-shot to the Unitree G1 humanoid robot, demonstrating agile, natural, and robust execution of the full soccer skill repertoire under real-world conditions.

ATHENA: Accelerated Multi-Task Heterogeneous Influence Functions for Robot Data Curation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Shanghai Innovation Institute / Shanghai Jiao Tong University

摘要原文

In robot imitation learning, influence functions provide a principled approach to quantify each demonstration's effect on robot task outcomes, yet scaling them to billion-parameter Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models is limited by computational and multitask bottlenecks. To this end, we propose ATHENA, an influence function framework tailored for multitask VLA data curation at billion-parameter scale. Concretely, it leverages the Kronecker structure of linear-layer gradients to reduce projection cost, and approximates dense Hessian inversion with a rank-$r$ Random Truncated Approximation, achieving about a 313.4$\times$ speedup in influence computation. Furthermore, ATHENA formulates global and local interactive influence to balance data curation across 50 jointly trained tasks. Extensive evaluations on RoboTwin 2.0 and real-robot deployment, covering 9.34 and 6.90 hours of demonstrations, respectively, show that ATHENA matches or exceeds full-data joint fine-tuning using only 50\% of demonstrations in simulation and 66\% of data across six real-robot tasks. Overall, ATHENA demonstrates its effectiveness for data curation in billion-parameter multitask VLA fine-tuning. The code will be released upon acceptance.
