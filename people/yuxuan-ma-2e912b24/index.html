本届研究与论文

Prioritized Multi-Constraint Coordination for Safe Multi-Robot Formation Navigation

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Safe formation navigation requires a robot team to reach task goals while avoiding collisions and maintaining a desired formation geometry. In this multi-robot cooperative task, task progress, collision avoidance, and formation maintenance often impose competing requirements on the team. Rather than treating these requirements as equally weighted objectives, we view them as prioritized constraints, where safety-critical constraints should take precedence over recoverable coordination errors such as temporary formation deformation. We formulate this setting as prioritized multi-constraint coordination and introduce a CTDE-based priority-conditioned actor--critic method. During execution, each agent uses a decentralized actor conditioned on local observations and two online budgets that describe the current safety and formation regimes. During training, a multi-head centralized critic shares a common representation and predicts navigation return, safety violation, and formation degradation through three value heads. These estimates are used to update the online budgets and compute a hierarchical advantage for policy optimization. This design enables the team to avoid collisions, temporarily deform the formation when safety requires it, recover the desired structure after local conflicts, and continue goal-directed navigation. We evaluate the method in VMAS-based formation-navigation scenarios with static and dynamic obstacles, and further demonstrate the learned policy on a fleet of omnidirectional AGVs.
