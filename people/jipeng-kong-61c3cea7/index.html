Graph-Reweighted Behavior Cloning with Human Interventions for Precise Robotic Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：China Telecom TeleAI / ShanghaiTech University

摘要原文

Long-horizon precise manipulation produces mixed-quality data for post-training vision-language-action (VLA) models: even successful demonstrations and intervention-assisted rollouts may contain detours, hesitations, failed attempts, and imperfect recoveries. The key challenge is to identify which action chunks actually advance the task, rather than uniformly cloning or discarding imperfect trajectories. We propose Graph-Reweighted Behavior Cloning (GR-BC), an offline iterative post-training framework that builds a task-conditioned goal-reachability graph over demonstrations and iteratively collected rollouts, including both successful and failed episodes with human interventions. Reverse graph search computes minimal remaining steps to task success within the dataset support, and each action chunk is weighted by how much its execution reduces this potential. This consequence-based weighting reuses recoverable segments from failed trajectories, leverages interventions as recovery paths, and down-weights locally regressive chunks even inside successful trajectories. New rollouts are incorporated by appending them to the graph and rerunning search, without training a reward model, action-conditioned critic, or progress predictor. Across four precise manipulation tasks and three VLA backbones, GR-BC improves single-round supervised fine-tuning and reaches 91% success after a few offline iterations, without trajectory comparisons or progress labels.

Pro-HOI: Perceptive Root-guided Humanoid-Object Interaction

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：China Telecom / ShanghaiTech University

摘要原文

Executing reliable Humanoid-Object Interaction (HOI) tasks for humanoid robots is hindered by the lack of generalized control interfaces and robust closed-loop perception mechanisms. In this work, we introduce Perceptive Root-guided Humanoid-Object Interaction, Pro-HOI, a generalizable framework for robust humanoid loco-manipulation. First, we collect box-carrying motions that are suitable for real-world deployment and optimize penetration artifacts through a Signed Distance Field loss. Second, we propose a novel training framework that conditions the policy on a desired root-trajectory while utilizing reference motion exclusively as a reward. This design not only eliminates the need for intricate reward tuning but also establishes root trajectory as a universal interface for high-level planners, enabling simultaneous navigation and loco-manipulation. Furthermore, to ensure operational reliability, we incorporate a persistent object estimation module. By fusing real-time detection with Digital Twin, this module allows the robot to autonomously detect slippage and trigger re-grasping maneuvers. Empirical validation on a Unitree G1 robot demonstrates that Pro-HOI significantly outperforms baselines in generalization and robustness, achieving reliable long-horizon execution in complex real-world scenarios
