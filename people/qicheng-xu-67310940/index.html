本届研究与论文

PIVOT: Policy Improvement via Trust-Region Optimization from Deployment Experience

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Post-training with real-world experience is critical for reliable deployment of robot foundation models, but heterogeneous data from demonstrations, human corrections, and autonomous rollouts are difficult to convert into stable policy updates. We present PIVOT, an iterative real-world post-training system built on Trust-Region Flow Matching Optimization (TRFO). TRFO provides a unified reinforcement-learning objective for heterogeneous deployment experience, using signed advantages to attract high-value transitions and repel low-value ones within a trust region while preserving the flow-matching training form. At each iteration, we first collect on-robot data using the current policy, estimate transition-level values via distributional TD learning, and then apply TRFO to update the policy. The updated policy is subsequently redeployed on the robot to gather the next round of experience, forming an iterative post-training procedure. On a suite of precise, contact-rich bimanual manipulation tasks, PIVOT yields consistent, monotonic gains over successive iterations, substantially improving success rates and throughput across all evaluated tasks.
