SEED-UMI: Sharing the Exoskeleton between human and robot for onE-to-one Dexterous demonstration

Poster · 共同一作 · 已核原文

该篇个人署名单位：Delta Intelligence

让人和机器人共享同一外骨骼，把示范记录转换成可配对的跨构型监督，并保持腕部相机观察几何一致。重点是接触丰富灵巧任务中的示范效率和人机映射误差。

摘要原文

Imitation learning for dexterous hands is bottlenecked by the difficulty of collecting contact-rich demonstrations that transfer faithfully to the robot. Prior wearable-exoskeleton systems record only on the human side and retarget via open-loop mappings calibrated in free space, which degrade under contact. We present SEED-UMI, a framework in which both the human and the robot wear the same exoskeleton: joint encoders become a physically shared measurement, and wrist cameras mounted to the exoskeleton observe the same outer mechanism during both human data collection and robot policy rollouts. This turns retargeting into paired cross-embodiment supervision and lets policies train directly on raw exoskeleton-centric wrist images, without segmentation or inpainting. On five contact-rich tasks, SEED-UMI achieves 3.0× greater data collection efficiency than exoskeleton-based teleoperation and a 70.0% average rollout success rate.
