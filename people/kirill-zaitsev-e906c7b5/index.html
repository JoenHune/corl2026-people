本届研究与论文

Z3P: Zero-Shot 3D-Object Perception for Robotics

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Tremendous progress has been made toward zero-shot, 3D-object-prompted perception tasks, including detection, segmentation, and 6D pose estimation. However, prior methods still struggle to see widespread use in robotics due to complexities such as challenging materials, dense clutter, and runtime. To address these limitations, we introduce Z3P, the first competitive end-to-end transformer architecture that performs zero-shot detection, segmentation, and pose estimation from single/multi-view RGB or RGB-D data and a 3D-object prompt. While previous methods use bounding boxes as an intermediate step between multiple models, Z3P operates as a single network that maintains an internal representation through persistent object tokens, allowing for better runtime and improved performance in densely cluttered environments with challenging materials. Across five robotic benchmarks (IPD, XYZ-IBD, ITODD-MV, T-ROBI, and ROBI), Z3P establishes a new state-of-the-art by becoming the first zero-shot method to outperform all prior zero-shot and object-specific approaches on detection and pose estimation. Finally, we demonstrate Z3P's utility as a foundation model for 3D-object perception, generalizing to multiple demanding robotic applications, including transparent object bin picking, sorting, car charging, and electronics assembly.
