本届研究与论文

Water-Filling Allocation and Phase Transitions in Action-Conditioned Multimodal Information Bottlenecks

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

Robots in contact-rich manipulation must decide which information to encode from vision and touch, while also determining the manipulation phases in which tactile information is sufficiently action-relevant to justify representation budget. We introduce Phase-Aware Variational Multimodal Information Bottleneck (PA-VMMIB), a visuo-tactile policy that suppresses tactile latents before contact and reallocates rate to touch during high-precision contact. The method is motivated by an Action-Conditioned Multimodal Information Bottleneck (AC-MMIB): within a modality-separated encoder class, and under explicit separability and conditional-noise assumptions, the analysis gives a contact-conditioned water-filling diagnostic in which active modalities equalize marginal rate-distortion slopes and the tactile branch activates only when contact makes touch action-relevant. A calibrated exponential-family approximation predicts an activation scale inversely related to the leading tactile Fisher mode, $\beta_c \propto 1/\lambda_{\max}(\mathbf{J}^{\tau})$; in smooth non-ideal tactile models, this hard threshold becomes a finite-width crossover. Across Linear-Gaussian checks, MuJoCo+TACTO tasks, and a Franka+DIGIT peg-in-hole setup, PA-VMMIB improves most in high-precision regimes and matches vision-dominated policies when touch is less decision-critical. On the physical task, it improves over vision-only by 26 percentage points at 4 mm clearance and converges toward vision-only performance at 8 mm, supporting phase- and precision-dependent tactile allocation as a practical design principle rather than a universal optimality claim.
