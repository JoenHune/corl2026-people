本届研究与论文

FR-VLA: Implicit Force Reasoning VLA for Contact-Rich Manipulation

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Nanyang Technological University

摘要原文

Integrating Vision-Language-Action Model (VLA) with force awareness is not merely a matter of encoding wrench measurements as an additional modality, but of determining the optimal timing and strength when and how physical interaction should influence contact-conditioned robot action generation. Existing force-aware VLAs typically treat force-to-action conditioning as a constant channel, leaving its decision relevance to be inferred implicitly during action prediction. We argue that force-informed action generation requires an intermediate force-relevance mechanism inside the VLA, where visual-language context estimates the task-dependent relevance of physical feedback before force is injected into the action pathway. FR-VLA addresses this gap with a VLM-based implicit force reasoning module that derives a vision-language-grounded force-admission signal from learnable force-query tokens and uses it to modulate an external tokenized force pathway for action generation. Real-world experiments on two robotic platforms and five contact-rich tasks show a 20.8% average success rate improvement over the strongest baseline, with visualizations confirming phase-dependent force admission dynamics.
