CoLA: Robust Collaborative Perception with Linear Global Spatial Alignment

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

Collaborative perception enhances perceptual accuracy and range through multi-agent information sharing. To address spatial misalignment caused by pose error and communication latency in collaborative perception, existing methods enhance robustness by extending adaptive local feature fusion regions. However, computational bottleneck of basic operators prevents local windows from being extended to the global scope, making it difficult to cover complex and diverse perception scenarios. Accordingly, this paper proposes CoLA, a robust collaborative perception scheme which achieves global spatial semantic matching and alignment across agents with linear computational complexity. CoLA integrates Global Structure Rectification Module for global structure modeling and inter-agent denoising, and Ego-Anchored Gate Fusion module for semantic alignment and fusion. Furthermore, we design a patch-level structural consistency loss to suppress severe position jitter. Extensive experiments on both real-world and simulated datasets demonstrate that CoLA outperforms state-of-the-art methods under various noisy conditions.
