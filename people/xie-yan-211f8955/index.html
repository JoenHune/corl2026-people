本届研究与论文

How to Build an Efficient VLA: A Design Space Study for Modular Vision-Language-Action Models

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models combine a pretrained vision encoder, a language backbone, and an action head, but no controlled measurement exists of which design choices actually drive performance. We fix the backbone (SigLIP2-So400m and Qwen2.5) and the pipeline (DROID, LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus), and train $63$ configurations that vary the action head along four axes (decoder, loss, initialization, inference passes) and the per-module scale, with measured RTX~5090 latency on every cell. Three findings come out of the experiment. First, the action head should be initialized from the last transformer layers of the language backbone; this one choice adds $7.1$ points at no latency cost and flips the preferred value of every other axis. Second, the action head is the highest-return module to scale, but only under that initialization. Third, returns saturate at a model size close to the one today's $\pi$-series VLAs already use, so growing the model further wastes compute. The three together specify \textsc{EffVLA}, a $\sim$3.75B model that matches the strongest open-source VLAs on standard LIBERO, leads on most LIBERO-Plus perturbation axes at lower latency, and transfers without modification to a real SO-ARM101 arm.
