OdorSpaces: Visual-Olfactory Embodied Navigation in 3D Environments

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Olfaction provides critical information about airborne chemical cues, yet it remains largely underexplored in embodied AI, with limited prior machine olfaction work focusing primarily on semantic odor perception. We introduce OdorSpaces, a physics-based simulation platform and benchmark for visual-olfactory embodied navigation. OdorSpaces augments Habitat-Sim with 3D odor transport and dispersion simulated by Fire Dynamics Simulator (FDS), enabling agents to navigate photorealistic indoor environments using both visual and olfactory observations. The benchmark includes two representative source-seeking scenarios: early-stage fire localization and invisible gas-leak localization. We develop an initial multimodal reinforcement learning policy that integrates visual observations and olfactory measurements over time. Experiments show that the policy substantially outperforms diverse baselines, establishing a strong reference for future research on visual-olfactory embodied navigation.

ControlTac: Scaling Tactile Data with Physically Controlled Tactile Image Generation

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Vision-based tactile sensing is widely used in perception, reconstruction, and robotic manipulation, yet collecting large-scale tactile data remains costly due to diverse sensor-object interactions and inconsistencies across sensor instances. Existing approaches to scaling tactile data—simulation and free-form tactile generation—often yield unrealistically rendered signals with poor transfer to highly dynamic real-world tasks. We propose ControlTac, a two-stage controllable tactile image generation framework that generates realistic tactile images conditioned on a single reference tactile image, contact force, and contact pose. By grounding generation in these important physical priors, ControlTac synthesizes realistic samples across different sensors while effectively capturing task-relevant variations. Across a series of downstream tasks and real-world experiments, such as object insertion, imitation learning, and object weighting, the augmented datasets using our approach consistently improve performance and demonstrate practical utility in dynamic real-world settings.

OmniTacTune: Policy-Agnostic Real-World RL for Tactile Residual Adaptation of Visual Policies

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of Maryland

摘要原文

Visual policies learned from human videos, teleoperation, and robot demonstrations offer scalable motion priors, but often fail in contact-rich manipulation, where success significantly depends on local force and contact geometry. Tactile sensing provides these complementary signals, yet tactile data remain costly to collect and hard to generalize across sensors, robots, and tasks. We introduce OmniTacTune, a policy-agnostic real-world RL pipeline that adapts tactile feedback to pretrained visual policies through residual correction. OmniTacTune uses a two-stage design: it first warm-starts tactile-aware learning from autonomous base-policy rollouts, then learns a lightweight tactile residual policy through online interaction. Extensive experiments show that OmniTacTune generalizes across diverse contact-rich tasks, visual base policies, and tactile representations. Across four real-world contact-rich tasks, it improves visual base policies from 5–40% success to 85–100% within 40–80 minutes, demonstrating an efficient path for adapting tactile feedback to scalable visual robot policies.
