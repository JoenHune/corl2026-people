本届研究与论文

Plann3r: Predicting Planning Costs Grounded in 3D

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Planning paths or trajectories for robot navigation requires understanding scene geometry and traversability. Classic approaches relied on accurate 3D maps to define occupancy based planning costs. Learning based alternatives predict planning costs either in terms of distance to goal or temporal distance between images. However, the former overfits to scene layout and the latter lacks geometric understanding. Moreover, most of these methods estimate \textit{image-level} scalar costs, which are not sufficiently informative to effectively guide the robot. In this paper, we propose \textbf{Plann3r} as a 3D grounded method to predict \textit{pixel-level} planning costs in terms of geodesic distances for any given set of images with an arbitrary subgoal pixel. We integrate Plann3r within a navigation pipeline, dubbed \textbf{VGGT-Nav}, in two unique ways: the offline mapping cum global planning phase iteratively uses Plann3r to generate subgoals and reference image global costmaps; and in the execution phase Plann3r performs simultaneous localization and local planning to generate planning costmaps to directly condition a learnt control policy. We show that the proposed Plann3r and VGGT-Nav largely outperform baselines respectively on the planning and navigation tasks on existing benchmark dataset. We further show real world demonstrations that highlight sim-to-real deployment. We will make the code publicly available: \url{https://plann3r.github.io/} (anonymized).
