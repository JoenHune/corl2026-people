本届研究与论文

Learning Non-Myopic Quadrotor Pursuit in Cluttered Environments via Differentiable Physics

Poster · 共同署名

摘要原文

Quadrotor pursuit-evasion in cluttered environments requires the pursuer to jointly track the evader and avoid obstacles. Policies trained with local distance or visibility rewards can become short-sighted: actions that reduce the current distance may still lead to blocked routes, unsafe maneuvers, or states with poor future capture feasibility. We propose a differentiable framework that trains non-myopic pursuit policies through closed-loop rollouts. Specifically, we introduce Global Reachability Value (GRV), a training-only value that evaluates future capture potential, guiding the policy toward obstacle-aware states that facilitate long-term success. Furthermore, to handle intermittent target observations, we introduce the Visibility-Gated Residual Belief (VGRB). This mechanism maintains target state estimation by coupling a Kalman motion prior with a visibility-gated residual predictor. In simulation validation, the proposed framework achieves 89.65%, 86.52%, and 76.04% capture rates with zero collision in three representative cluttered scenarios. Ablations show that GRV reduces myopic route-selection failures, while VGRB improves target-state estimation under occlusion. Experiments in Gazebo and real-world further validate the superiority of our framework.
