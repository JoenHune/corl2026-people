本届研究与论文

Benchmarking Real-World Robotic Manipulation through Stochastic Challenges

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

A challenging bottleneck in manipulation research is reproducibility and comparability of methods in real-world environments. Since manipulation is an interactive task, it cannot be evaluated on a static dataset of observations. Benchmarking in simulation is a common remedy, but cannot cover the whole complexity of real-world tests. This paper proposes a new paradigm or stochastic challenges. Like the well-known YCB objects, it is based on reproducible real-world tasks and therefore allows system-level innovation and comparability with different platforms. Due to their stochastic nature, our challenges however mimc the riguour and surprise of hidden test sets. To do this, we show how different robots in different labs can be compared based on their success rate over different random task samples, if these samples come from the same distribution. We propose stochastic challenges as a general paradigm to robot benchmarking and implement this paradigm in a novel benchmark for mobile manipulation, on which we compare two robots tested in two different labs.
