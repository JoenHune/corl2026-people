本届研究与论文

CS-Pi: Constraint-Satisying Flow Policy Sampling

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

A flow-matching policy deployed in the field must often satisfy a constraint that was absent from its demonstrations, such as a collision-free path or a particular grasp orientation. Existing training-free samplers take one of two approaches. \emph{Guidance} steers the denoising trajectory with a constraint gradient at noisy intermediates: the gradient at a noisy sample need not point toward a clean feasible one. \emph{Projection} maps the finished sample onto the feasible set and executes it: it is efficient, but the result is an action the policy would never draw. We find that this dichotomy need not hold. Our key insight is that the projection is a cheap, clean, feasible signal, and the only mistake is executing it. We introduce Constraint-Satisfying Flow Policy Sampling \texttt{CS-Pi}: we draw from the policy, project the clean sample to the nearest feasible point, and resample from the flow model biased toward that point. Iterating drives the samples toward constraint satisfaction with no noisy-state gradient and no auxiliary network; the method extends to arbitrary costs with a single tweak in the projection function. We evaluate on a 2D diagnostic, a 7-DoF shelf pick-and-place, and a zero-shot Franka deployment. In every setting, \texttt{CS-Pi} stays closest to the policy while satisfying the constraint, and it attains the highest safe-success on a real-world obstacle avoidance task.
