本届研究与论文

Perfect Demo Makes Poor Teacher:Learning Robust Alignment from Critical Motion Segments

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Zhejiang University

摘要原文

Expert demonstrations are often assumed to be the gold standard for robot imitation learning, yet for fine-grained manipulation tasks such as insertion, stacking, and alignment, we find a counterintuitive failure mode: fluent expert demonstrations can be poor teachers. Skilled teleoperators compress the most critical alignment and recovery behaviors into a brief temporal window, leaving imitation models dominated by redundant free-space motion and starved of supervision on the high-precision segments that actually determine success. We study this phenomenon through the lens of critical motion segments and propose three complementary remedies: (i) a deliberate demonstration strategy that slows down near alignment and exposes corrective micro-adjustments, (ii) a resampling scheme that upweights key segments around gripper state changes and task-critical transitions, and (iii) a dynamic feature representation that compresses short temporal neighborhoods of visual observations into predictive features, conditioning the action expert on local motion and alignment progress rather than isolated static frames. Together, these results show that robust robotic alignment benefits less from perfect trajectories than from demonstrations and representations that expose the structure of correction, pointing toward a more pedagogical view of robot data collection where demonstrations are optimized not only for human efficiency but for machine learnability.
