本届研究与论文

PlayWorld: Learning Robot World Models from Autonomous Play Data

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Princeton University

摘要原文

Action-conditioned video models ("world models") are emerging as a promising foundation for data-driven robot simulators that learn world dynamics directly from data. Yet, the role of training data remains poorly understood in shaping their accuracy and reliability. While existing approaches typically train on human demonstrations or task-specific policy rollouts, they exhibit persistent hallucinations in modeling contact-rich manipulation due to systematic biases and insufficient coverage of the training distribution. To address this gap, we present PlayWorld, a new data paradigm for training robot world models from autonomous play. Rather than relying on manual teleoperation or narrow policy rollouts, PlayWorld enables robots to autonomously explore their environments, capturing a broad spectrum of object dynamics and behavioral modes. The resulting world models can simulate diverse policy behaviors with high physical fidelity. We show that PlayWorld enables fine-grained failure prediction and policy evaluation, achieving up to 40% improvements over human-collected data baselines. Using the learned world model for reinforcement learning yields policies that achieve 65% higher real-world success rates upon deployment. To our knowledge, PlayWorld is the first work to demonstrate autonomous play as an effective training paradigm for video world models.
