AnyRank: Reliable Anytime Policy Ranking via Sequential Testing

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：University of Pennsylvania

摘要原文

Due to the high cost of hardware experiments, robot policies are predominantly evaluated by comparing their empirical performance on a limited number of hardware trials. However, finite-sample statistical uncertainty can lead to erroneous conclusions, especially when ranking multiple policies using a fixed batch of evaluation data. We propose \algname: a \emph{sequential} method for policy ranking that (i) \emph{adaptively allocates trials} to hard-to-separate policies, (ii) provides (partial) rankings at \emph{any stopping time} while bounding the probability of an incorrect ranking, and (iii) optionally \emph{exploits prior information} to prioritize hardware evaluations more efficiently using a small set of held-out hardware evaluations or rollouts in a world model. We achieve these properties through novel theoretical contributions that combine sequential and multiple-hypothesis testing. Experiments in simulation and real-world policy evaluation benchmarks show that \algname requires up to 50% fewer hardware evaluations compared to existing statistically rigorous approaches, while guarding against the incorrect rankings produced by heuristic methods used by state-of-the-art benchmarks.
