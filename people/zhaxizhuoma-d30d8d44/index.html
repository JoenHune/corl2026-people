本届研究与论文

VISTA: Vision-Grounded and Physics-Validated Adaptation of UMI data for VLA Training

Poster · 共同署名

该篇个人署名单位：Shanghai Jiao Tong University

摘要原文

Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI) enables scalable data collection without target-robot teleoperation, but UMI-style demonstrations are not directly suited for large-scale Vision-Language-Action (VLA) training. We identify the challenge of training VLA models from UMI-collected data as a dual adaptation problem: (i) wrist-mounted fisheye observations are misaligned with the visual distributions of pretrained vision-language models, and (ii) human-collected trajectories are not guaranteed to be executable under target-robot embodiment constraints. To mitigate this challenge, we present VISTA, a framework that adapts UMI-collected data to VLA training through fisheye-aligned visual grounding and embodiment-conditioned trajectory validation. UMI-VQA supplies vision-language supervision under wrist-mounted fisheye observations, while trajectory validation scores human-collected demonstrations by continuity, self-collision risk, and execution fidelity to select physically plausible action supervision before training. VISTA then uses the adapted visual-language data and validated trajectories in a two-stage VLA training procedure. Experiments show that UMI-VQA improves policy performance over action-only training and standard-view VQA, while validation scores are predictive of real-robot deployment success. Across UMI-style simulation and real-world manipulation tasks, VISTA consistently improves over strong VLA baselines including $\pi_{0.5}$, LingBot-VLA, and Wall-X. We will release the pipeline, datasets, and pretrained model.
