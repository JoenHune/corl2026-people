本届研究与论文

Telescopes are Optomechanical Robots: Training Compositional Visuomotor Policies for Coordinated Control

Poster · 首位署名

摘要原文

Direct imaging of Earth-like exoplanets requires $10$ milliarcsecond angular resolution and $10^{-9}$ contrast; engineering a single telescope that simultaneously exhibits the aperture baseline and wavefront control needed to meet both requirements has proven challenging. We build on recent advances in distributed-aperture interferometer designs to recast the problem as a high-dimensional visuomotor optomechanical robotic control task: many physically separate mirror segments must be aligned to within a small fraction of the observing wavelength, given only science path focal plane imagery. We train recurrent visuomotor policies with PPO to phase such a telescope end-to-end from the interferometric focal-plane image. Direct application of these methods to the full forty-five-DOF, fifteen-segment problem is ineffective; instead we train fifteen per-segment alignment skill policies, each articulating three actuators, and compose them at inference into a single $240$-step rollout. The same policy architecture, with a target-conditioning input and a modified reward, is able to perform directed wavefront shaping to suppress flux in designated dark-hole regions by approximately two orders of magnitude across many target regions. A coarse-to-fine variant of the same pattern co-phases a two-segment aperture from $2$ arcsec initial differential motion in a median of fifteen control steps, approximately five orders of magnitude faster than an online-optimization baseline.
